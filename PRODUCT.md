## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (modern design system tokens, CSS grid/flexbox, zero dependencies), lightweight vanilla ES6 JavaScript for mobile navigation, accessible modals, and interactive equipment calculators. No bloated frameworks or unnecessary external CSS libraries.

## Users

1. **Plant Engineers & Maintenance Heads:** Technical decision-makers managing factories, textile mills, CNC machine shops, plastic injection molding units, and automotive plants across Tamil Nadu and South India. They need exact CFM, dew point, TR, pressure ratings, power consumption, and dimensions within seconds.
2. **Industrial Procurement Managers & Buyers:** Looking for direct factory pricing, ISO certifications, rapid RFQ quotation turnaround, reliable warranties, and local service support in Coimbatore/Tamil Nadu.
3. **OEM Project Consultants & HVAC Contractors:** Sizing integrated cooling loops and compressed air treatment skids; looking for engineering drawings, P&ID schematics, and downloadable PDF catalogues.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the digital engineering headquarters and high-converting commercial front for Win Equipments. The website establishes Win Equipments as a serious, established, precision industrial equipment manufacturer with in-house fabrication, rigorous factory acceptance testing (FAT), and deep engineering expertise. It turns technical plant requirements into qualified, high-intent RFQs, phone calls, and WhatsApp enquiries.

## Positioning

Win Equipments is a direct engineering manufacturer based in Arasur, Coimbatore, with 18+ years of expertise (established 2008). Unlike commercial traders or catalogue resellers:
1. Direct factory manufacturer with integrated engineering works in Arasur, Coimbatore.
2. "Save Water and Power" engineering philosophy: specifically tropicalized systems rated for 45°C ambient temperatures and 50°C inlet temperatures common in Indian summers.
3. Project consultancy approach: custom engineering (e.g. specialized soda chillers, medical MRI/CT chillers, anodizing acid chillers, multi-cell FRP cooling towers) with 100% FAT run testing and 1.5x hydrostatic pressure certification.

## Product Principles

1. **Engineering Credibility Over Marketing Fluff:** Lead with verified specs, operating principles, design conditions, and real equipment photography. Never look like a generic corporate template or an AI landing page.
2. **Mobile is First-Class:** Every page, card, specification, and form must be effortless to read and operate on 320px–430px screens with zero horizontal overflow, clear typography, and comfortable thumb-friendly tap targets.
3. **Instant Clarity & Scannability:** Visitors must understand what Win Equipments manufactures, its key ratings, and how to enquire within 3 seconds of landing on any page.
4. **Frictionless Engineering Inquiry:** Clear, contextual CTAs ("Request Factory Quote", "Talk to Engineer", "WhatsApp RFQ", "Download Catalogue") that capture plant requirements without friction.
5. **Architectural Purity & Fast Performance:** Clean semantic HTML, modular CSS tokens, minimal JS, zero layout shift (CLS), and lightning-fast loading speeds on mobile networks.
