Why is clean dry air essential for CNC machine shops in Hosur? Modern CNC lathes, Vertical Machining Centers (VMCs), and Horizontal Machining Centers (HMCs) rely on compressed air for automatic tool changers (ATC), spindle air purges, and pneumatic clamping. Moisture droplets in air lines wash away critical lubricant from solenoid valves, causing valve seizure, pneumatic actuator sluggishness, and costly machine tool downtime. Win Equipments refrigerated dryers deliver continuous +3°C pressure dew point air, extending pneumatic valve life by over 3x.
