Why choose Win Equipments process chillers for Chennai factories? Industrial plants in Chennai's automotive belts (Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Mahindra World City) operate continuous 3-shift production under severe tropical ambient temperatures exceeding 43°C. Standard commercial HVAC chillers frequently fail due to undersized heat rejection and high head pressure trips. Win Equipments chillers feature oversized condensers engineered with hydrophilic coated fins, dual independent refrigeration circuits for 50% backup redundancy, and food/pharma-grade SS304 internal hydraulic circuits.
