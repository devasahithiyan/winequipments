Why do textile mills in Tiruppur experience compressed air moisture failures? Tiruppur mills operate continuously in high humidity with ambient temperatures exceeding 38°C to 42°C. At this temperature, a 100 CFM screw compressor generates up to 18 liters of water per 8-hour shift. Without an engineered +3°C pressure dew point refrigerated dryer, this liquid moisture travels down header pipes directly into air-jet loom main nozzles, autoconer electronic yarn clearers, and pneumatic splicers, causing expensive yarn breakage and production downtime.
