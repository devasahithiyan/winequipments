Installing and Servicing Refrigerated Air Dryers in Tamil Nadu Factories

Expert installation and maintenance services for industrial air dryers in Coimbatore and beyond.

Tamil Nadu presents a unique challenge for refrigeration equipment: Reliability at 45°C ambient temperatures. Win Equipments doesn't just sell boxes; we sell installed performance.

The Installation Checklist

Proper installation accounts for 80% of a machine's lifespan. Here is what we mandate for every install:

  1. Room Ventilation: The dryer removes heat from the air. If the room is small and unventilated, that heat builds up, tripping the dryer. Exhaust fans are mandatory.
  2. Pre-Filter with Auto Drain: Essential to stop compressor oil from coating the dryer's heat exchanger.
  3. Bypass Line: A 3-valve bypass allows us to service the dryer without stopping your factory production.
