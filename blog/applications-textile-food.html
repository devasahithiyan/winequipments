Applications of Refrigerated Air Dryers in Textile and Food Processing

Why clean, dry air is critical for textile mills and food safety compliance.

Different industries have different reasons for drying air. In Tirupur and Coimbatore, the focus is largely on Textile and Food. Here is how our dryers serve these sectors.

1. The Textile Industry (Spinning & Weaving)

Textile mills use compressed air for auto-coners, splicing, and air-jet looms.

The Problem

If water droplets reach the splicing nozzle, the yarn splice becomes weak or stained. In Air Jet looms, moisture alters the drag on the weft insertion, causing loom stoppages.

Win Equipments Solution: We provide dryers with high-capacity moisture separators to handle the high humidity in textile halls. Result: Increased Loom Efficiency by 8-12%.

2. Food Processing & Packaging

Compressed air is used for "Puffing" bags, operating sorting pistons, and cleaning conveyors.

The Risk: Moisture = Mold. If wet air sits in food grade pipes, bacteria grow. This can cause a batch recall.

Compliance: ISO 8573 Class 4 (attained by our dryers) ensures air is dry enough to inhibit microbial growth for indirect contact applications.

3. Plastic Injection Molding

Dry air is used to convey granules and operate molds. Moisture causes surface defects and weakness in the final plastic part.
