Maintenance Tips for Refrigeration Air Dryers in Hot, Humid Climates

Essential maintenance practices to keep your dryer running efficiently in Tamil Nadu summers.

In high-ambient regions like Tamil Nadu, air dryers work overtime. Dust and heat are the biggest killers of refrigeration systems. Here is a simple maintenance schedule to ensure long life for your equipment.

Daily Checks (Operator Level)

  • Check Drain Operation: Press the test button on your automatic drain valve. Ensure water is actually coming out. If only air comes out, it is working. If nothing comes out, the drain might be clogged.
  • Inlet Temperature: Touch the inlet pipe. It should not be burning hot (above 55°C). If it is, check your compressor's aftercooler.
  • Dew Point Indicator: Ensure the gauge is in the green zone or showing approx +3°C.

Weekly Maintenance

Clean the Condenser Fins

This is the #1 specific task. The condenser radiator gets clogged with dust, cotton fluff (in textile mills), or oil mist.
How to clean: Use compressed air to blow OUTWARD from the inside of the fin pack (reverse flow). Do not use water unless necessary, and never bend the fins.

Monthly Maintenance

  • Check Pre-Filter: Ensure the pre-filter element isn't clogged. A high differential pressure will starve the dryer of air. Replace if the indicator turns red.
  • Inspect Fan Operation: Ensure the cooling fan cycles on/off properly or runs continuously depending on the model. Listen for noise in the fan motor.

Annual Service (Technician Level)

Once a year, call a Win Equipments certified technician to:

  1. Check refrigerant gas pressure (suction/discharge).
  2. Test the compressor amperage (current draw).
  3. Deep clean the condenser with chemical coil cleaner.
  4. Calibrate the Hot Gas Bypass Valve.
Warning: bypass lines

We often see customers open the "Bypass Valve" during a dryer failure and forget to close it after repair. Always tag your valves! An open bypass renders the dryer useless.
