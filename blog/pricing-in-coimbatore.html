Refrigerated Air Dryer Pricing in Coimbatore: What to Expect

A transparent look at the costs of air dryers and what factors influence the price.

Procurement managers in Coimbatore often ask for the "Best Price" immediately. While price is critical, buying an air dryer is an investment in your plant's reliability. Here is a realistic guide to pricing in the Tamil Nadu industrial market.

Market Price Ranges (Estimates)

Note: These are indicative market ranges for quality industrial dryers (Stainless Steel Heat Exchangers), not cheap aluminum imports.

Capacity (CFM) Typical Application Price Range (INR)
20 - 40 CFM Small Workshop / CNC ₹25,000 - ₹45,000
50 - 100 CFM Small Factory / Painting ₹55,000 - ₹95,000
150 - 300 CFM Textile Mill / Food Process ₹1.2 Lakhs - ₹2.5 Lakhs
500+ CFM Large Heavy Industry ₹3.0 Lakhs - ₹5.5 Lakhs

Why is there a Price Difference?

You might find a trader offering a 50 CFM dryer for ₹35,000 while a branded OEM quotes ₹60,000. Why the gap?

  • Heat Exchanger Material: Cheap dryers use Aluminum plate fins which corrode in 2 years. We use Copper Tube-in-Tube exchangers which last 15+ years.
  • Compressor Brand: We use Emerson/Danfoss. Generic units use re-conditioned or Chinese commands.
  • Condenser Size: Our units are tropicalized (oversized) for Indian summers. Cheap units trip when the temp hits 40°C.
The "Hidden" Cost:

A cheap dryer with a high pressure drop (0.5 bar) will cost you approx ₹30,000 per year in extra electricity. That "savings" on purchase price is wiped out in year one.
