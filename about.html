Our Story

Established in 2008 in the industrial hub of Arasur, Coimbatore, Win Equipments began with a singular mission: to provide Indian industries with cooling solutions that could withstand the rigors of tropical climates and continuous operation.

Under the leadership of our proprietor, Mr. Ramasamy Ananthakumar, we have grown from a small manufacturing unit into a comprehensive solution provider. We understand that in sectors like textiles and food processing, downtime is costly. That's why every chiller and cooling tower we build is engineered for maximum reliability.

Today, we are proud to be an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, exporting our "Made in India" excellence to global markets including Kuwait, Oman, and Jordan.
