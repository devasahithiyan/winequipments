Who is Win Equipments? Win Equipments is an ISO 9001:2015 accredited capital equipment manufacturer founded in 2008 in Arasur, Coimbatore, India. Operating under the guiding principle "Save Water and Power", the company engineers and fabricates industrial process chillers (1 to 150 TR), FRP counterflow and crossflow cooling towers (10 to 1500 TR), and refrigerated compressed air dryers (20 to 2000 CFM) for textiles, plastic injection molding, CNC machining, fiber lasers, and heavy industrial plants worldwide.

Our Philosophy

Engineered for Tropical Climates & Continuous 24/7 Duty

Indian manufacturing shop floors face severe ambient operating realities: summer temperatures topping 45°C, high-TDS borewell water causing aggressive mineral scaling, and heavy particulate loads in foundries and textile mills.

Standard off-the-shelf imported equipment degrades rapidly under these conditions. Under the leadership of Mr. Ramasamy Ananthakumar, Win Equipments designs every chiller, cooling tower, and air dryer with oversized heat transfer surfaces, virgin isophthalic FRP resins, and commercial-grade hermetic scroll compressors to guarantee continuous, uninterrupted performance.

Our registered manufacturing plant in Arasur features integrated fabrication bays, pneumatic pressure testing stations, hydro-testing facilities up to 1.5x working pressure, and strict multi-stage quality assurance before dispatch.
