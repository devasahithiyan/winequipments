Inside The Machine: Engineered for 45°C Ambients

Click or hover over components to explore the engineering that gives Win Equipments systems a 15+ year operational lifespan under continuous 24/7 industrial duty.

1
2

4

1 Danfoss & Copeland Hermetic Scroll Compressors

Engineered for high condensing temperatures up to 50°C. Delivers 20% lower specific energy consumption compared to reciprocating compressors, with zero valve flutter.

2 Dual-Pass Stainless Steel / Copper Heat Exchanger

Counter-current thermodynamic design maximizes heat transfer surface area with less than 0.2 bar total differential pressure drop across full CFM airflow.

3 Capacitive Zero Air Loss Condensate Discharge

Solid-state capacitive level sensor evacuates condensate without venting compressed air, eliminating power waste and saving ₹38,000+ per year per machine.

4 Microprocessor Diagnostic Controller & Dew Point Monitor

Continuous digital dew point readouts, safety trips (high/low pressure, anti-freeze), and automated fan cycling to prevent evaporator freeze-up at partial load.

5 Integrated 0.01μ Borosilicate Coalescing Stage

Multi-layer borosilicate media traps liquid water, sub-micron oil aerosols, and pipe rust before compressed air reaches pneumatic tools and robotic valves.
