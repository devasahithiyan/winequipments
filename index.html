About Win Equipments

Welcome to Win Equipments, a name synonymous with reliability in the Indian industrial utility sector. Established in 2008 in the industrial heartland of Arasur, Coimbatore, we have grown from a visionary startup into a distinguished manufacturer, supplier, and exporter of high-grade Compressed Air and Water Cooling Systems.

Under the leadership of our proprietor, Mr. Ramasamy Ananthakumar, Win Equipments has cultivated a culture of engineering precision. We understand that in industries like textiles, automotive, and food processing, utility downtime is not an option. That is why our products are engineered to withstand the rigorous demands of continuous industrial operation.
