Product Overview

Engineering Excellence in Air Treatment

Win Equipments Refrigerated Air Dryers deliver reliable moisture extraction to safeguard downstream pneumatic machinery, instrumentation, and finished goods. Utilizing advanced 3-in-1 aluminium plate heat exchangers, our systems achieve optimal heat transfer with minimal pressure drop.

✓ 3-in-1 Compact Heat Exchanger

Combines air-to-air heat exchanger, evaporator, and moisture separator into a single brazed aluminium block, drastically cutting energy consumption.

✓ Stable +3°C Dew Point

Precision hot gas bypass valve maintains constant dew point control across fluctuating plant loads, preventing moisture condensation in air lines.

✓ Eco-Friendly Refrigerants

Charged with non-ozone depleting R-134a and R-407C refrigerants conforming to international environmental safety standards.

✓ Zero-Loss Electronic Drain

Microprocessor-controlled automatic condensate drain ensures condensate discharge without compressed air wastage.

Thermodynamic Working Principle

Warm, moisture-laden compressed air enters the air-to-air precooler where it is precooled by outbound chilled air. It then passes into the refrigerant evaporator where cooling to +3°C causes moisture vapour to condense into liquid droplets. The high-efficiency integrated demister separates condensed water and oil, discharging them via the automatic drain valve. The dry air is reheated before exit, ensuring zero pipe sweating.

Built for Rigorous Industrial Environments

Heavy-duty epoxy powder-coated steel casing resists corrosion in humid factory environments. Designed for ambient operating temperatures up to 50°C and inlet air temperatures up to 60°C.
