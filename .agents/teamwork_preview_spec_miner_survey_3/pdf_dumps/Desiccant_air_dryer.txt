Heatless Twin-Tower Desiccant Air Dryers
Ultra-Deep Moisture Adsorption Delivering Guaranteed -40°C to -70°C Pressure Dew Point
Engineered for Sub-Zero -40°C Pressure Dew Point (ISO 8573-1 Class 2): Completely
prevents line freeze-ups, pipeline corrosion, and moisture condensation in
pharmaceutical cleanrooms, robotic paint booths, laser cutting heads, and electronic
assembly lines.
The Win Equipments WHD Series utilizes dual vertical pressure vessels filled with
premium spherical activated alumina desiccant. Operating on a precision 8-minute
Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) cycle, one tower adsorbs moisture under pressure
while the opposing tower regenerates using an expanded dry air purge stream (12–15%).

STANDARD PRE & AFTER FILTRATION PACKAGE
To guarantee -40°C performance and safeguard desiccant longevity, Win Equipments supplies pre-piped upstream coalescing pre-filters (0.01 micron /
0.01 mg/m³ oil carryover) and downstream particulate after-filters (1.0 micron) with differential pressure indicators.
Dew Point Dependent Switching (DDS) Option: Optional online capacitive ceramic dew point sensor extends drying cycles when inlet air is lightly
loaded, reducing purge air consumption from 15% down to under 5% during low pneumatic demand periods.
