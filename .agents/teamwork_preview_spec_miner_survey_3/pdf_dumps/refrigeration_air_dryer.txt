High-Efficiency Non-Cycling Direct-Expansion Dryers Engineered for Extreme Tropical Ambient Climates
Engineered for Constant +3°C Pressure Dew Point (ISO 8573-1 Class 4): Protects
downstream pneumatic machinery, automated assembly cylinders, textile autoconers,
and laser cutting heads from liquid moisture, rust fouling, and line freeze-ups.
Win Equipments WRD Series utilizes an advanced 3-in-1 modular heat exchanger
combining an air-to-air regenerator, direct-expansion evaporator, and moisture demister
in a single compact housing. Pre-cooling incoming air with outgoing cold dry air lowers
compressor power consumption by up to 40% while preventing distribution pipe
sweating.
