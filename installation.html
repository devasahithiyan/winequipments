Factory Certified Engineers

Turnkey Installation & Commissioning Support

Proper mechanical alignment and thermal balancing are essential to achieving rated efficiency. Win Equipments deploys certified factory engineers to oversee complete erection, piping, and trial commissioning.

What does Win Equipments turnkey installation include? Our on-site installation service covers civil foundation verification, equipment positioning with anti-vibration mounts, header piping connection with isolation bypass valves, electrical motor starter wiring, pressure leak testing at 1.5x operating pressure, and trial run thermal balancing to guarantee designed water flow (LPM) and dew point (+3°C).

Field Execution Standard

The 4-Stage Commissioning Protocol

Step 01

Foundation & Site Survey

Inspection of plinth level, anchor bolt positioning, ventilation clearance, and drainage gutters before equipment offloading.

Step 02

Mechanical & Piping Hookup

Flange alignment, vibration isolator connection, water header loop integration, and bypass valve manifold installation.

Step 03

Hydro-Testing & Pressure Proofing

Leak proofing at 1.5x working pressure, flushing sediment from pipework, and dry nitrogen purging of refrigerant joints.

Step 04

Trial Run & Handover

Full-load thermal pull-down trial, micro-controller programming, operator SOP training, and final warranty sign-off.
