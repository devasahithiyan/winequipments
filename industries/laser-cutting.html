Why do fiber laser machines require dual-circuit chillers? Fiber laser cutting machines generate intense heat at two distinct temperature thresholds. The fiber laser power source (diodes) requires chilled water at 22°C (±0.5°C) to prevent thermal degradation and beam fluctuation. However, if 22°C water is piped to the cutting head in humid Indian summer conditions, ambient moisture immediately condenses on the optical protective windows, causing lens cracking and beam distortion. A dual-circuit chiller supplies 28°C water to the optics head, staying above dew point while keeping the laser source chilled.
