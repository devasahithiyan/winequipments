How do chillers and cooling towers benefit plastic injection molding plants? In plastic injection molding, cooling accounts for over 70% of the entire mold cycle time. Operating two separate water circuits is the industrial best practice: 1. Chilled Water Loop (10°C to 15°C): Circulated through core/cavity cooling channels to rapidly solidify molten polymer, reducing mold cycle time by up to 35% and preventing part warping. 2. Cooling Tower Water Loop (32°C): Circulated through the machine's hydraulic oil cooler, keeping oil viscosity below 50°C to extend hydraulic pump life and seal integrity.
