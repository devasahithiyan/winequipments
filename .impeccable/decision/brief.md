# Win Equipments — site (homepage + product template)

Scope: whole marketing site, rebuilt mobile-first. Homepage and family hubs are Persuade; product spec explorer, selector tools and quote form behave as Operate.
Audience: plant maintenance heads on phones on hot, bright factory floors; procurement and consultants on desktop comparing model tables.
Job: understand what Win makes within seconds, find the right model, request a quote / WhatsApp / call.
Proof on hand: real product cut-outs, factory photos, catalogue datasheets (only source of numbers), ISO 9001:2015, since 2008, 1,200+ installations, confirmed client logos.
Constraints: no invented claims, prices, ratings or case studies; existing product URLs kept; #008810 brand accent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site plots what the equipment does. Air and water move from a hot 45°C Indian plant to a guaranteed dew point or leaving temperature, drawn as real engineering charts with catalogue numbers. Refuses the IndiaMART slider template and the dark glass "engineering studio" look.

OWN-WORLD: Chart-paper ground (#F6F7F3) with true measuring rulings only where a chart sits, ink #15201A, one green #008810 process line that also marks every primary action, a deep blue #1D4E89 saturation/reference curve, hairline rules #C8D0CA. Condensed engineering grotesk display with tabular figures for every number; quiet sans body; real product photography on paper ground; square-cornered controls, 1px rules, no cards-in-rows, no glass, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees the problem (hot wet air, process heat), sees Win's equipment bring it to spec on a chart, trusts it because the numbers are real and the factory is real, finds their model by capacity, and requests a quote with the model attached.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: compact header (logo, call icon, Menu); H1 naming dryers, chillers and FRP cooling towers built in Coimbatore; one supporting line; Request Quote (green, full-width) plus WhatsApp; then the chart plate filling the width: 45°C saturated inlet falling along the green process line to +3°C pressure dew point, state points labelled. Desktop: copy column left (5/12), chart plate with product photo right (7/12), credibility line beneath. Signature interaction: the duty-point selector, type a CFM or TR and the capacity axis lights the covering model with its quote link; on phones a vertical capacity axis rules the model ladder. Raises: persistent design-conditions readout on product pages; one full flat #008810 enquiry field; stage-by-stage labelled working-principle diagrams; addressable model rows (#model) carried into quote links.

FORM: The Dew-Point Chart, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key c9957d4a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
