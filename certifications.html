Our 5-Stage Pre-Shipment Quality Testing Protocol

No machine leaves our Arasur assembly floor without completing 100% rigorous validation against standard engineering benchmarks.

1.5x Hydrostatic Pressure Test

Every air receiver tank and chiller shell & tube heat exchanger is hydrostatically pressure tested to 1.5 times working pressure in accordance with IS 2825 / ASME Section VIII.

Dry Nitrogen Refrigerant Leak Detection

Refrigeration circuits in air dryers and chillers are pressurized with dry nitrogen at 350 PSI for 24 hours followed by electronic halogen leak detection.

Dynamic Aerodynamic Balancing

All cast aluminum cooling tower fan impellers and drive shafts are dynamically balanced to ISO 1940 Grade 2.5 standards to eliminate vibration and bearing wear.

Full-Load Electrical & High-Pot Testing

Control panels undergo megger insulation testing, phase sequence verification, overload relay calibration, and temperature controller sensor offset testing.

Need Quality Inspection Reports or Mill Test Certificates?

We supply third-party inspection dossiers, material test certificates (MTC), and factory acceptance test (FAT) documentation on request for EPC projects.
