Accurately determine the exact refrigerated or desiccant dryer capacity required for your compressor with ISO 8573 pressure and temperature derating factors.

How do you size a compressed air dryer? To size a compressed air dryer, divide your compressor's Free Air Delivery (CFM) by three correction factors: Pressure Factor (Cp), Inlet Temperature Factor (Ct), and Ambient Temperature Factor (Ca): $$\text{Required Dryer CFM} = \frac{\text{Compressor Nominal CFM}}{C_p \times C_t \times C_a}$$ In tropical climates like South India, high ambient and inlet temperatures derate dryer capacity significantly, requiring a 20% to 40% larger dryer model to maintain a +3°C pressure dew point.
