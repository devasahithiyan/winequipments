How do you calculate industrial chiller tonnage? Industrial chiller tonnage is calculated using the thermodynamic heat transfer equation: $$\text{TR} = \frac{\text{Flow (LPM)} \times \Delta T (^\circ\text{C}) \times 4.186}{211}$$ Where $\text{Flow}$ is in liters per minute, $\Delta T$ is $(T_{\text{inlet}} - T_{\text{outlet}})$, $4.186 \text{ kJ/kg}^\circ\text{C}$ is the specific heat of water, and $211 \text{ kJ/min}$ equals 1 Ton of Refrigeration (TR). A 15% safety factor is standardly added for high Indian ambient conditions.
