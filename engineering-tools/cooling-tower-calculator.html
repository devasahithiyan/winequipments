Calculate exact cooling tower tonnage (TR), approach temperature, range, and evaporation water consumption in seconds.

How do you calculate cooling tower approach and range? The Cooling Range is the temperature difference between hot water entering the tower and cold water leaving: $\text{Range} = T_{\text{hot}} - T_{\text{cold}}$. The Approach is the temperature difference between cold water leaving the tower and the ambient wet-bulb temperature: $\text{Approach} = T_{\text{cold}} - T_{\text{wb}}$. An approach between 3°C and 5°C is standard for high-efficiency industrial FRP cooling towers in Indian industrial conditions.
