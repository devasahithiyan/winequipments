    Cooling Towers

    Cooling towers reject process heat to the atmosphere. FRP towers cool water by evaporation to wet bulb + 4 °C; coil towers keep water sealed in a finned coil and cool it to ambient + 4 °C.

    • Round FRP Cooling Towers10–300 TR

    Sized from real engineering data

    Compressed air leaves the compressor saturated. A WRD dryer chills it from 45 °C to a +3 °C pressure dew point, and 92% of the water vapour condenses and drains away.

    What does Win Equipments manufacture?

    Compressed air treatment equipment (refrigerated and desiccant air dryers, filters, automatic drain valves, air receivers and aftercoolers), industrial process chillers, and FRP and coil cooling towers, plus spares. Everything is made at our works in Arasur, Coimbatore.

    Where are you located?

    Our works are at SF No. 4/195 B, Kallangadu, Nadu Arasur, Arasur Post, Coimbatore 641 407, Tamil Nadu.

    How do I get a quotation?

    Send your requirement through the enquiry form, WhatsApp +91 95972 28969 or email info@winequipments.com. Include your capacity (CFM, TR or water flow), operating pressure and plant conditions so our engineers can size the equipment.

    Which refrigerated dryer suits my compressor?

    Start with the compressor's actual capacity in CFM and correct it for inlet temperature, ambient temperature, pressure and dew point using the factors on the refrigerated dryer page. The calculator there does this for you.
