    Troubleshooting a refrigerated air dryer

    By Win Equipments engineersUpdated 3 October 20262 min read

    Most "dryer not working" calls turn out to be a drain, a bypass valve or a dirty condenser. Work through these checks before calling for service.

    Water downstream of the dryer

    Possible causeWhat to check
    Drain not dischargingPress the drain's test button. Clean the strainer. Check the power supply to timer drains.
    Bypass valve openWith a three-valve bypass, the bypass valve must be closed and the inlet and outlet valves open.
    Water was already in the pipesPipework wet from before the dryer was fitted keeps releasing water for a while. Drain the low points.
    Dryer overloadedCompare actual air flow and conditions with the dryer's rating using the sizing calculator.

    Dew point reads high

    Possible causeWhat to check
    Dirty condenserClean the fins; check the condenser fan runs.
    Inlet air too hotWRD dryers accept up to 60 °C. Check the compressor aftercooler.
    Hot roomThe dryer needs cool ventilation air. Check the room temperature against the 35 °C rating and the correction factors.
    Refrigerant problemNeeds a technician to check pressures.

    Drain not discharging

    • Press the test button and listen for the discharge.
    • Isolate the drain and clean the strainer, which often blocks with rust and scale.
    • On timer drains, check the interval and discharge time settings.

    Ice or frosting inside the dryer

    The hot gas bypass valve keeps the evaporator above 5 °C so condensate cannot freeze. Frosting suggests it needs adjustment or has failed. This is a technician's job.

    Dryer will not start or keeps tripping

    • Check supply voltage. WRD single-phase models accept 180 to 260 V; three-phase models 380 to 420 V.
    • A high-pressure trip usually follows a blocked condenser or a failed fan. Clean and check before resetting.
    • The controller's on-delay timer protects the compressor, so a short wait after power-on is normal.

    If the problem persists, call or WhatsApp us with the dryer model and what you see on the controller. We supply spares and service for our equipment.
