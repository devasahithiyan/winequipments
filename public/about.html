    What we do

    One team from the first enquiry to after-sales support.

    • Design and sizing

      Our engineers select the model from catalogue ratings and correction factors, or engineer a special unit around your duty.

    • Manufacturing

      Fabrication and assembly at our works in Arasur, Coimbatore. Chillers are factory tested; air receivers are hydrostatically tested.

    • Project consultancy

      We design, plan and commission the system around the equipment: piping, filtration, drains and controls.

    • Service and spares

      After-sales service and spares for the equipment we supply, from cooling tower fills to refrigeration parts.
