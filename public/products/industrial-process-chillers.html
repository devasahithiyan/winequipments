    How it works

    The chilling plant has two circuits: a refrigeration system (compressor, condenser, fan, expansion device and evaporator) and a water circulating system (pump, motor and tubing).

    1. Water in

      Process water returns to the chiller through the water inlet.

    2. Evaporate

      In the brazed plate heat exchanger (evaporator) the water is cooled by the refrigerant. Counter-flow achieves the maximum temperature difference with minimum pressure drop.

    3. Reject heat

      The refrigerant compressor sends the absorbed heat to an air- or water-cooled condenser.

    4. Water out

      Cooled water is pumped from the closed stainless steel tank back to the process at the set temperature.

    Brazed plate heat exchanger

    • Sized to keep fluid velocities and turbulence high
    • Counter-flow for maximum temperature difference
    • Minimum pressure drop

    Refrigeration compressor

    • Hermetically sealed reciprocating or scroll

    • Low noise, low power consumption

    Circulating pump

    • Stainless steel impeller, mechanical seal
    • Rated for 24-hour duty
    • Motor overload protection

    Tank

    • Non-corrosive insulated stainless steel tank
    • Closed tank: no heat gain
    • Removable steel covers for access

    Controls

    • Programmed temperature controller with alarms
    • On-delay protects the compressor
    • High / low pressure gauges and switches

    Hot gas bypass valve

    • Prevents evaporator temperature dropping below 5 °C
    • Manual or automatic

    Questions engineers ask

    What two values do I need to select a chiller?

    The required water outlet (cold) temperature, and the heat load to be removed. Heat load Q = mass flow (LPM) × ΔT between inlet and outlet temperature.

    Are WCP chillers air cooled or water cooled?

    Every WCP model is available with either an air-cooled or a water-cooled condenser.

    What is the tank made of?

    Up to WCP 075 the tank is stainless steel. WCP 100 to WCP 200 are offered in stainless or mild steel.

    How does ambient temperature affect capacity?

    Capacity is rated at 40 °C ambient. At 45 °C apply a factor of 0.9, at 50 °C 0.8.
