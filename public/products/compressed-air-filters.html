    Filter grades

    Each WMF housing takes any of the four colour-coded elements. Install them in series to reach the air class your process needs.

    GradeParticle removalOil removalInitial pressure dropMediaEfficiencyISO 8573-1
    GP3 micron—0.03 bar gPolypropylene—Particle class 3 · oil class 5
    ZP0.1 micron0.1 mg/m³0.05 bar gBorosilicate99%Particle class 1 · oil class 3
    ZO0.01 micron0.01 mg/m³0.06 bar gBorosilicate99.99%Particle class 1 · oil class 2
    ZC0.01 micron0.005 mg/m³0.12 bar gBorosilicate with impregnated activated carbon99.9999%Particle class 1 · oil class 1

    How it works

    Filters are installed in series so each grade protects the next and the air reaches the class your process needs.

    1. GP

      General purpose: removes particles down to 3 micron ahead of the dryer.

    2. ZP

      Removes particles to 0.1 micron and oil to 0.1 mg/m³.

    3. ZO

      High-efficiency coalescing to 0.01 micron and 0.01 mg/m³ oil.

    4. ZC

      Activated carbon removes oil vapour and odour to 0.005 mg/m³.

    Questions engineers ask

    Which filter grade do I need?

    Use GP (3 micron) as a pre-filter, ZP (0.1 micron) for general protection, ZO (0.01 micron, 0.01 mg/m³ oil) for high-quality air, and ZC (activated carbon) where oil vapour and odour must be removed.

    When should elements be replaced?

    Replace the element when the differential pressure reaches 0.7 bar g.

    What pressure can WMF filters handle?

    All grades are rated to a maximum operating pressure of 30 bar g and 80 °C.
