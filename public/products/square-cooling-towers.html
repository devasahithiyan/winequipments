    How it works

    Hot water from the process is distributed over the fills; air drawn through by the fan evaporates a small fraction and cools the remainder.

    1. Spray

      Spray nozzles distribute water evenly through a wide spray angle across the fill pack.

    2. Film

      Water runs over honeycomb PVC fills as a thin film with a large contact surface.

    3. Evaporate

      The axial fan draws air through the fills; evaporation cools the water.

    4. Eliminate drift

      PVC drift eliminators catch entrained droplets and reduce carry-over loss.

    5. Collect

      Cooled water collects in the basin and is pumped back to the process.

    Questions engineers ask

    Round or square tower: which should I choose?

    Both use the same evaporative principle and capacity range. Square towers suit tight plots, rooftops and installations against walls; round towers use a rotary sprinkler instead of spray nozzles.

    What outlet water temperature can I expect?

    Outlet water is rated at wet bulb temperature + 4 °C.

    Which fill should I choose?

    L fills for inlet water up to 55 °C, H fills for 55–85 °C, and polypropylene rings (P) for hot or dirty water.
