    WDV Series

    Even a small compressed air system produces large quantities of condensate laced with oil. WDV automatic drain valves discharge it from receivers, filters, dryers and pipelines without shutting the system down and without wasting compressed air.

    How it works

    Condensate carried with the air to end-use points is harmful if the system is not drained regularly. An automatic drain removes it without losing excessive compressed air and without shutting down the system.

    1. Collect

      Condensate collects at the low point of a receiver, filter, dryer or pipeline drop leg.

    2. Strain

      A strainer-cum-manual shut-off valve protects the drain and allows isolation for service.

    3. Discharge

      The valve opens on its timer interval, controller signal or float level and discharges the condensate at high velocity without clogging.

    Typical installation

    1. Air receiver drain
    2. Filter drain
    3. Refrigerated dryer drain
    4. Pipeline drop leg

    What goes into it

    Electronic timer

    No air loss

    • Discharges condensate only
    • No power required in float type

    Questions engineers ask

    Which drain valve suits my system?

    Use WDV M12 or L40 up to 50 CFM, H12 or H40 from 51 to 500 CFM, and B16, ZA16 or F16 up to 1000 CFM. Choose the 40-series for pressures up to 40 kgf/cm².

    What is a no-air-loss drain?

    The WDV F16 discharges only condensate, so no compressed air is wasted, and it needs no electrical supply.

    Can I get a low-voltage version?

    Yes. Standard supply is 230 V AC; 24 V, 48 V and 110 V AC or DC versions are available to order.
