    Compressed Air Treatment

    Dryers, filters, drains and receivers for dry, clean compressed air.

    • Refrigerated Air Dryers

      10–2000 CFM · WRD Series Removes moisture from compressed air by chilling it to a +3°C pressure dew point. View range

    • Heatless Desiccant Air Dryers

      300–2000 CFM · WHD Series Twin-tower dryers that adsorb moisture for −20 °C to −40 °C pressure dew points. View range

    • Compressed Air Filters

      40–1700 CFM · WMF Series Coalescing filters in four grades that remove oil, dirt, odour and water. View range

    • Automatic Drain Valves

      Up to 1000 CFM · WDV Series Timer, micro-controller and no-air-loss drains that clear condensate automatically. View range

    • Air Receiver Tanks

      250–10,000 L · WRV Series Hydro-tested pressure vessels that store air and settle condensate. View range

    • Aftercoolers

      Engineered to order Aftercoolers with moisture separator that cool compressor discharge air. View range

    • Moisture Separators

      Engineered to order Separators that remove condensed water from compressed air after the aftercooler. View range

    Process Cooling

    Chillers that hold process water at a set temperature.

    • Industrial Process Chillers

      0.5–20 TR · WCP Series Holds process water at a set temperature with a closed stainless steel tank and pump. View range

    • Medical Scan Chillers

      Engineered to order Chillers engineered to order for the cooling circuits of MRI and CT scanners. View range

    • Anodizing Chillers

      Engineered to order Bath chillers engineered to order for anodizing and electroplating lines. View range

    • Acid Cooling Chillers

      Engineered to order Chillers engineered to order for acids and corrosive process fluids. View range

    • Ice Flake Machines

      Engineered to order Flake ice machines sized to your daily ice requirement. View range

    • Shell & Tube Heat Exchangers

      Engineered to order Heat exchangers built to order to transfer heat between two fluids without mixing them. View range

    • Milk & Coconut Water Chillers

      Engineered to order Chillers built to order to cool milk, coconut water and other liquid foods. View range

    • Soda Chillers

      Engineered to order Chillers built to order to cool water and drinks for soda and beverage production. View range

    • Condensing Units

      Engineered to order Compressor, condenser and controls on one base, assembled to order for your evaporator. View range

    • Air-Cooled Chillers

      0.5–15 TR packaged · Engineered to order Packaged chillers that reject heat to air through condenser fans, so no cooling tower is needed. View range

    Cooling Towers

    FRP and coil towers that reject process heat to atmosphere.

    • Round FRP Cooling Towers

      10–300 TR · WCT RL Series Round bottle-type FRP towers that reject process heat by evaporation. View range

    • Square FRP Cooling Towers

      10–300 TR · WCT SL Series Square-footprint FRP towers with spray nozzles and drift eliminators. View range

    • Coil Cooling Towers

      40–280 TR · WCC Series Finned copper-coil dry coolers that keep process water sealed from the air. View range

    • Spares & Consumables

      Spares Fills, fans, motors, sprinklers, compressors, valves and filter elements. View range

    • Cooling Tower Fills

      Spares PVC fill packs, drift eliminators, nozzles, PP rings and fan blades for FRP cooling towers. View range

    • Fanless Cooling Towers

      Engineered to order Natural draft towers with no fan, for hot water from gensets, furnaces and compressors. View range
