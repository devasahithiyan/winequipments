    Cooling Tower PVC Fills, Drift Eliminators and Fans

    Spares

    Fills are where a cooling tower does its work: hot water spreads over thin PVC sheets so air can take the heat away. When fills clog with scale or break down, the tower stops reaching its design temperature. We supply replacement PVC fills, drift eliminators, spray nozzles, PP rings and fan blades for FRP towers.

    Questions engineers ask

    What size are your cooling tower fills?

    Our standard PVC fill pack is 600 × 300 × 150 mm, with 0.2 to 0.25 mm sheet and a flute height of 12 to 19 mm, in black or blue PVC.

    How do I know the fills need replacing?

    The tower stops reaching its design outlet temperature, water channels through in streams instead of spreading, or the fill packs are visibly scaled, sagging or broken. Send a photo and the tower details and we will confirm.

    What does a drift eliminator do?

    It catches water droplets that the fan air would otherwise carry out of the tower, cutting water loss and wet surroundings. Ours are rigid PVC packs, 600 × 600 × 120 mm, for square towers.

    Do you supply fills for towers made by other companies?

    Send the fill size, type and a photo of what is fitted now. If our standard packs match or can be cut to fit, we will quote.
