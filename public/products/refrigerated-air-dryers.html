    How it works

    Air contains dust, moisture and traces of oil. Untreated wet compressed air causes equipment failures and contaminates the production process, so it must be dried before use.

    1. Pre-cool

      Saturated compressed air enters the pre-cooler / re-heater, where the outgoing cold air pre-cools it and reduces the heat load on the evaporator.

    2. Chill

      In the evaporator the air is cooled further to the required dew point. The refrigerant compressor and condenser supply low-temperature refrigerant to the evaporator.

    3. Separate

      Cooling reduces the air's capacity to hold water vapour; the moisture condenses and is removed by the water separator and automatic drain.

    4. Re-heat

      The cold, dry air is re-heated as it passes back through the air-to-air heat exchanger before leaving the dryer.

    5. Match load

      The thermostatic expansion valve and hot gas bypass valve match the refrigerant system to the compressed air cooling load, keeping the dew point constant.

    What goes into it

    Show all 6 build details

    Co-axial heat exchanger

    • Acts as pre-cooler and evaporator
    • Copper tubes in a coiled tube-in-tube arrangement
    • Fully encapsulated in PUF insulation

    Refrigeration compressor

    • Hermetically sealed reciprocating or scroll compressor
    • Suitable for eco-friendly gases
    • Low noise and low power consumption

    Thermostatic expansion valve

    • Selected for the cooling load and operating condition
    • Holds a constant dew point at varying load
    • Responds to suction pressure and temperature

    Temperature controller

    • Programmed micro-controller
    • Indicates dew point, inlet and condensing temperature with alarms
    • On-delay timer protects the compressor

    Hot gas bypass valve

    • Fitted between compressor discharge and evaporator
    • Prevents evaporator temperature dropping below 5 °C
    • Manual or automatic

    Condenser

    • Air-cooled: aluminium plate-finned cross-flow exchanger
    • Water-cooled option on larger models
    • Spigot construction lowers pressure drop and compressor power

    Questions engineers ask

    What dew point does a WRD dryer deliver?

    WRD dryers deliver a +3 °C pressure dew point and hold it at varying load using a thermostatic expansion valve and hot gas bypass valve.

    How do I size a refrigerated dryer for my compressor?

    Divide your compressor's actual capacity by the product of the four correction factors for inlet temperature, ambient temperature, inlet pressure and dew point. Then choose the WRD model at or above that nominal capacity. The sizing tool on this page does the calculation for you.

    Which models run on single-phase power?

    Models with an S suffix (WRD 20 S to WRD 250 S) run on 230 V single phase. T models from WRD 300 T upward run on three-phase supply.

    Is a water-cooled condenser available?

    Yes. WRD 600 T and larger are offered with air- or water-cooled condensers.
