    Air-Cooled Water Chillers

    Applications

    Air-cooled units suit plants where water is scarce or hard. In hot sites a water-cooled chiller with a cooling tower can use less power; see the comparison guide.

    • Injection moulding and plastic machines
    • Hydraulic presses and oil cooling
    • Compressor cooling
    • Food, milk and beverage cooling
    • Sites with poor water supply or no space for a cooling tower

    Questions engineers ask

    What is an air-cooled chiller?

    A chiller whose condenser is cooled by air from fans instead of water from a cooling tower. It needs no cooling tower, condenser pump or water treatment, which makes it simpler to install.

    What sizes of air-cooled chiller do you make?

    Packaged air-cooled water chillers from 0.5 to 15 TR with a built-in tank and pump. For example, our 1 TR unit is rated 3,000 kcal/hr and our 2 TR unit 6,200 kcal/hr. Larger units are built to order.

    How much does an air-cooled chiller cost?

    As listed on our IndiaMART store on 5 October 2026: a 1 TR air-cooled WCP chiller ₹55,000 to ₹78,000, and a 2 TR chiller for injection moulding ₹85,000. Your price depends on capacity, temperature and site conditions.

    Air-cooled or water-cooled: which should I choose?

    Air-cooled is simpler and needs no water; water-cooled with a cooling tower usually uses less power in hot climates and suits larger loads. Our guide on air-cooled vs water-cooled chillers compares them.
