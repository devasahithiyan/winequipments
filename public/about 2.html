    About Win Equipments

    We design, build and commission compressed air treatment equipment, process chillers and cooling towers at our works in Arasur, Coimbatore. Founded in 2008 by Mr. Ramasamy Ananthakumar.

    Manufacturer, supplier and project partner

    Win Equipments was established in 2008 as a manufacturer of compressed air and water cooling systems. We make the equipment, and we also take on project consultancy: designing, planning and commissioning the system around it.

    Our equipment is fabricated and assembled at our works in Arasur by the team pictured above. After commissioning we support each installation with service and spares.

    • Compressed air treatment: refrigerated and desiccant dryers, filters, drain valves, air receivers and aftercoolers
    • Process cooling: WCP process chillers and chillers engineered for anodizing, acid, medical scan and ice flake duties
    • Heat rejection: round and square FRP cooling towers and coil cooling towers

    Installation and service

    From the first enquiry to commissioning and after-sales support, one team at our Arasur works handles the job.

    1. 1

      Share your requirement

      Compressor CFM, heat load in TR or water flow, temperatures and site conditions. A drawing or nameplate photo helps.

    2. 2

      We size the equipment

      Our engineers select the model from catalogue ratings and correction factors, or engineer a special unit.

    3. 3

      Built at Arasur

      Fabricated and assembled at our works in Arasur, Coimbatore.

    4. 4

      Tested before dispatch

      Chillers are factory tested before delivery; air receivers are hydrostatically tested.

    5. 5

      Commissioned and supported

      We plan and commission the system, then provide after-sales service and spares.
