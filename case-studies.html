Tiruppur Export Knitwear: Eliminating Moisture-Induced Loom Downtime

Location: Mangalam Road, Tiruppur | Equipment: Win 500 CFM High-Ambient Refrigerated Dryer + Micro-Filters

Payback Period
4.2 Months

The Operational Challenge

A premier Tiruppur garment exporter operating 24 high-speed Tsudakoma pneumatic air-jet looms suffered frequent unexpected stops. During summer peak ambient temperatures (40°C–42°C with 65% RH), condensate and oil aerosols condensed inside the loom manifold. Relay valves seized, yarn weft insertion failed mid-flight, and oil stains ruined delicate knit rolls, generating ₹45,000 weekly in scrap loss.

Win Equipments Engineering Solution

Win Equipments conducted an on-site pressure and dew point audit. We replaced an undersized 300 CFM competitor unit with a Win 500 CFM High-Ambient Refrigerated Air Dryer (WAD-500) engineered with an oversized R134a condenser rated for 48°C ambient, paired with a 3-stage pre/fine/carbon coalescing filtration system (0.01 micron) and zero-loss capacitive automatic drain valves.

Hosur Tier-1 Auto Supplier: Precision Spindle Thermal Drift Elimination

Location: SIPCOT Industrial Complex, Hosur | Equipment: Win 15 TR Dual-Circuit Process Chiller (WAC-15D)

Tolerance Accuracy
±2 Microns

The Operational Challenge

Manufacturing high-precision transmission pinion shafts for commercial vehicles requires strict dimensional tolerance (±5 microns). As the CNC spindle temperatures rose from 24°C at shift start to 58°C during peak afternoon continuous grinding, thermal elongation induced a ±14 micron drift, triggering high CMM inspection rejections and frequent operator tool recalibration stops.

Win Equipments Engineering Solution

Win Equipments engineered a 15 TR Dual-Circuit Process Chiller incorporating dual Danfoss hermetic scroll compressors and an integrated SS304 insulated 350-liter buffer reservoir. A digital PID microprocessor system continuously regulates supply temperature at 20°C ±0.5°C regardless of load swings, routed through high-head centrifugal pumps to cooling jackets.

Plastic Injection Molding

Coimbatore Packaging Molder: 3.8s Cycle Time Reduction & Capacity Boost

Location: Kalapatti Industrial Estate, Coimbatore | Equipment: Win 30 TR Water-Cooled Chiller + 40 TR FRP Cooling Tower

Production Gain
+20.8% Yield

The Operational Challenge

A rigid polypropylene container manufacturer operating seven 250T Haitian injection molding machines faced severe mold cooling bottlenecks. Using only cooling tower water at 32°C in summer, total shot cycle time was 18.2 seconds. Cooling represented 65% of the cycle, leading to warped containers, uneven wall shrinkage, and inability to meet expanding FMCG delivery contracts.

Win Equipments Engineering Solution

Win Equipments separated the thermal circuit into a high-temperature circuit (oil cooler powered by our 40 TR FRP Round Tower at 30°C) and a precision mold-cooling circuit powered by our 30 TR Water-Cooled Industrial Chiller (WWC-30) maintaining 12°C chilling water directly through the mold cores via a 3.5 bar stainless booster pump.

Erode Dyeing Plant: 7+ Years Corrosion-Free Service with 1,800 ppm TDS

Location: Perundurai SIPCOT, Erode | Equipment: Win 150 TR Round Bottle FRP Cooling Tower (WCT-150)

Asset Longevity
7+ Years

The Operational Challenge

In textile dyeing and finishing plants across Erode, process cooling water contains high total dissolved solids (TDS 1,600 to 2,200 ppm) along with trace chemical fumes and salt carryover. The client's previous galvanized steel cooling tower experienced severe perforation within 26 months, while scaling fouled sprinkler nozzles and forced monthly shutdown cleanouts.

Win Equipments Engineering Solution

Win Equipments engineered an ultra-durable 150 TR FRP Round Bottle Cooling Tower (WCT-150) molded with premium grade isophthalic polyester resin, gel-coat UV barriers, and SS304 structural hardware. We deployed thermoformed non-clog honeycomb PVC fills with wide-flute channels and a low-pressure self-rotating brass sprinkler head that eliminates dynamic clogging.

Rigorous Quality Assurance

The Arasur QC Testing Bay: Validated Before Dispatch

Unlike assemblers who ship unverified equipment directly to site, every chiller, air dryer, and cooling tower at Win Equipments undergoes mandatory factory testing at our Arasur, Coimbatore facility before packing and crating.

1.5x Hydrostatic Pressure Test

All heat exchangers, air receiver tanks, and condenser coils are hydrostatically proof tested to 1.5 times operating design pressure (up to 24 bar) with zero pressure decay over 60 minutes.

Electronic Halogen Leak Sniffing

Refrigeration loops (R134a / R410A / R407C) are vacuumed below 200 microns and sniffed with high-sensitivity electronic sensors capable of detecting micro-leaks under 3 grams/year.

4-Hour Full-Load Thermal Run

Chillers and dryers are connected to thermal load banks at our test bay and run at 100% capacity for 4 continuous hours to log amp draw, discharge pressure, and thermodynamic dew points.

Certified Factory Test Report

Every delivered machine arrives with an authorized Quality Inspection Certificate signed by our QC Lead, documenting serial numbers, test parameters, Megger insulation ratings, and warranty.
