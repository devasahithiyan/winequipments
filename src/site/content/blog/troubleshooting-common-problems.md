Most "dryer not working" calls turn out to be a drain, a bypass valve or a dirty condenser. Work through these checks before calling for service.

## Water downstream of the dryer

| Possible cause | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Drain not discharging | Press the drain's test button. Clean the strainer. Check the power supply to timer drains. |
| Bypass valve open | With a three-valve bypass, the bypass valve must be closed and the inlet and outlet valves open. |
| Water was already in the pipes | Pipework wet from before the dryer was fitted keeps releasing water for a while. Drain the low points. |
| Dryer overloaded | Compare actual air flow and conditions with the dryer's rating using the [sizing calculator](/engineering-tools/air-dryer-sizing.html). |

## Dew point reads high

| Possible cause | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Dirty condenser | Clean the fins; check the condenser fan runs. |
| Inlet air too hot | WRD dryers accept up to 60 °C. Check the compressor aftercooler. |
| Hot room | The dryer needs cool ventilation air. Check the room temperature against the 35 °C rating and the correction factors. |
| Refrigerant problem | Needs a technician to check pressures. |

## Drain not discharging

- Press the test button and listen for the discharge.
- Isolate the drain and clean the strainer, which often blocks with rust and scale.
- On timer drains, check the interval and discharge time settings.

## Ice or frosting inside the dryer

The hot gas bypass valve keeps the evaporator above 5 °C so condensate cannot freeze. Frosting suggests it needs adjustment or has failed. This is a technician's job.

## Dryer will not start or keeps tripping

- Check supply voltage. WRD single-phase models accept 180 to 260 V; three-phase models 380 to 420 V.
- A high-pressure trip usually follows a blocked condenser or a failed fan. Clean and check before resetting.
- The controller's on-delay timer protects the compressor, so a short wait after power-on is normal.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is there water downstream of my air dryer?
Usually the drain is not discharging, the bypass valve is open, the pipework was already wet before the dryer was fitted, or the dryer is overloaded for the actual air flow and conditions.

### Why does my air dryer show a high dew point?
Common causes are a dirty condenser or failed condenser fan, inlet air hotter than the dryer accepts (up to 60 °C for WRD), a hot room without cool ventilation, or a refrigerant problem that needs a technician.

### Why is my air dryer freezing or frosting inside?
The hot gas bypass valve keeps the evaporator above 5 °C so condensate cannot freeze. Frosting suggests it needs adjustment or has failed, which is a technician's job.

### Why does my air dryer keep tripping?
Check the supply voltage (180 to 260 V for single-phase WRD models, 380 to 420 V for three-phase). A high-pressure trip usually follows a blocked condenser or a failed fan; clean and check before resetting.
