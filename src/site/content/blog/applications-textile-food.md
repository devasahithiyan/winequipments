Around Coimbatore and Tiruppur, textile, food and plastics plants are among the biggest users of compressed air. Each needs dry air for a different reason.

## Textile mills

Spinning, winding and weaving machines use compressed air for splicing, cleaning and, on air-jet looms, weft insertion. Water in the air can mark yarn and fabric, corrode valves and upset the air jets.

**Typical set-up:** a refrigerated dryer sized for the mill's summer conditions, with a pre-filter ahead of it and automatic drains on the receiver, filters and dryer. Textile halls are dusty with fibre, so keep the dryer's condenser clean. See [textile mills](/industries/textile-mills.html) for the equipment we supply.

## Food, dairy and beverage plants

Compressed air drives packaging machines, actuators and conveying, and in some places it touches the product or packaging. Wet air in pipework encourages corrosion and microbial growth.

**Typical set-up:** a refrigerated dryer for general plant air, with ZP and ZO coalescing filters, and a ZC activated carbon filter where odour and oil vapour must be removed. Where the air is used in very dry or cold areas, a [desiccant dryer](/products/desiccant-air-dryers.html) gives −20 °C or −40 °C. See [food, dairy and beverage](/industries/food-dairy-beverage.html).

## Plastics and injection moulding

Air operates mould actuators, part ejection, blow-off and conveying. Water causes corrosion in actuators and can spot finished parts.

**Typical set-up:** a refrigerated dryer with pre-filter and drains. Plastics plants also rely on [process chillers](/products/industrial-process-chillers.html) for mould cooling and [cooling towers](/products/round-cooling-towers.html) for the hydraulics; see [plastics and injection moulding](/industries/plastic-molding.html).

## What the air quality should be

Which ISO 8573-1 class you need depends on your equipment and customers. Our guide to [ISO 8573-1 purity classes](/blog/iso-8573-1-compressed-air-purity-classes.html) shows which dryers and filters reach each class.

## Sizing for summer

All three industries run hot plants. Dryer capacity falls as inlet and ambient temperatures rise, so size with the [air dryer sizing calculator](/engineering-tools/air-dryer-sizing.html) using your hottest conditions.
