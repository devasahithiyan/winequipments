## Heat exchangers

Shell and tube heat exchangers are engineered to order. A mild steel unit rated 8 bar starts at about ₹50,000; the price depends on duty, materials and design pressure.

## What changes the price

- **Capacity at your conditions.** Catalogue ratings assume standard conditions. Hot plants, high wet bulb or high inlet temperatures need a larger unit.
- **Materials.** Stainless steel or cast iron instead of mild steel, copper coils, or a special refrigerant change the price.
- **What is included.** Pumps, tanks, filters, drains, controls, installation and commissioning may or may not be part of the quote.
- **Taxes and freight.** Check whether GST, packing and delivery to your site are included.
