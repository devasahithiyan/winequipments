A dryer only performs to its rating when it is installed properly. This checklist covers the points we look at when we commission a system.

## Location

- **Ventilation.** A refrigerated dryer rejects heat through its condenser. Install it where it gets cool, fresh air, not in a closed room or next to the compressor's hot exhaust. Dryer capacity falls as ambient temperature rises above the 35 °C rating.
- **Access.** Leave room to clean the condenser and to service the drain and filters.
- **Supply.** Check the voltage before connecting: single-phase WRD models accept 180 to 260 V, three-phase models 380 to 420 V.

## Piping order

A typical layout from the compressor:

1. Compressor with aftercooler
2. [Air receiver](/products/air-receiver-tanks.html) with automatic drain
3. Pre-filter (GP grade) with drain
4. [Refrigerated dryer](/products/refrigerated-air-dryers.html)
5. Finer filters (ZP, ZO, ZC) as your air quality needs
6. Plant header

Use pipe sizes at least as large as the dryer's connections to avoid extra pressure drop.

## Bypass

Fit a three-valve bypass (inlet, outlet and bypass valves) so the dryer can be isolated for service without stopping production. Label the bypass valve and keep it closed in normal running.

## Drains

Every point that collects condensate needs a drain: receiver, filters, dryer separator and pipeline drop legs. An [automatic drain valve](/products/automatic-drain-valves.html) with a strainer and manual shut-off makes servicing easy.

## Before start-up

- Check inlet air temperature is within the dryer's range (10 to 60 °C for WRD).
- Power the dryer before opening the air, so it reaches dew point before air flows.
- Check the controller readings and that the drain discharges.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where should a refrigerated air dryer be installed?
In a well-ventilated place with cool, fresh air, not in a closed room or next to the compressor's hot exhaust. Dryer capacity falls as ambient temperature rises above the 35 °C rating. Leave room to clean the condenser and service the drain and filters.

### What is the correct piping order for a compressed air dryer?
Compressor with aftercooler, then air receiver with automatic drain, then a GP pre-filter with drain, then the refrigerated dryer, then finer filters (ZP, ZO, ZC) as your air quality needs, then the plant header.

### Why does an air dryer need a bypass?
A three-valve bypass (inlet, outlet and bypass valves) lets you isolate the dryer for service without stopping production. Keep the bypass valve closed in normal running.

### What should be checked before starting a new air dryer?
Check the inlet air temperature is within range (10 to 60 °C for WRD dryers), power the dryer before opening the air so it reaches dew point first, and confirm the controller readings and that the drain discharges.
