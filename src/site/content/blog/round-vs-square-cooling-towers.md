Round and square WCT towers work on the same evaporative principle and cover the same 10 to 300 TR range, with cold water rated at wet bulb + 4 °C. The differences are in how they distribute water and how they fit your site.

## Side by side

| | Round (WCT RL) | Square (WCT SL) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | Bottle type | Square footprint |
| Water distribution | Rotary sprinkler, spun by jet recoil | Spray nozzles with a wide spray angle |
| Drift control | Sprinkler design minimises drift | PVC drift eliminators (600 × 600 × 130 mm) |
| Fills | Honeycomb PVC fills (L or H), or PP rings | Honeycomb PVC fills (L or H), or PP rings |
| Capacity range | 10 to 300 TR | 10 to 300 TR |
| Suits | Open plots, single towers | Tight plots, rooftops, against walls, rows of towers |

## When to choose a round tower

The bottle-type round tower is the familiar choice for an open site with space around it. Its rotary sprinkler spreads water evenly over the fills without separate nozzles.

## When to choose a square tower

A square tower packs neatly against walls and alongside other towers, so it makes better use of a rectangular plot or rooftop. Several square towers can stand in a row for larger loads.

## Everything else is the same

Both shapes use gel-coated FRP casings, dynamically balanced axial fans and TEFC IP55 motors, and both are offered with L, H or P fill variants to suit the hot water temperature. Sizing is identical: see [range, approach and wet bulb](/blog/cooling-tower-range-approach-wet-bulb.html) or use the [cooling tower calculator](/engineering-tools/cooling-tower-calculator.html), which lets you choose either shape.

## Water that must stay sealed

If the cooling water must not be exposed to air, for example diesel engine jacket water, a [coil cooling tower](/blog/coil-cooling-towers-for-diesel-gensets.html) keeps it inside a finned coil.
