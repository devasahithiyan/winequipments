A process chiller keeps water at a set temperature so moulds, presses, baths and machines run consistently. Inside, it has two separate circuits that meet in one heat exchanger.

## Two circuits

- **The water circuit.** A pump circulates your process water from the chiller's tank, through the process, and back through the evaporator.
- **The refrigeration circuit.** A refrigeration compressor, condenser, expansion device and evaporator move heat out of the water and reject it to the air (or to cooling water).

## The cycle, step by step

1. **Water in.** Warm process water returns to the chiller.
2. **Evaporate.** In the brazed plate heat exchanger (the evaporator) the refrigerant boils and absorbs heat from the water. Counter-flow gives the largest temperature difference with the least pressure drop.
3. **Reject heat.** The refrigeration compressor sends that heat to an air-cooled or water-cooled condenser.
4. **Water out.** Cooled water is pumped from the closed stainless steel tank back to the process at the set temperature.

## What each part does in a WCP chiller

| Component | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Brazed plate heat exchanger | The evaporator: cools the water, sized for high turbulence and low pressure drop |
| Refrigeration compressor | Hermetic reciprocating or scroll; pumps the refrigerant around the circuit |
| Condenser and fans | Rejects the heat to air; water-cooled condensers are also available |
| Circulating pump | Stainless steel impeller, mechanical seal, rated for 24-hour duty |
| Tank | Closed, insulated stainless steel tank, so the water gains no heat |
| Temperature controller | Programmed controller with alarms; an on-delay protects the compressor |
| Hot gas bypass valve | Stops the evaporator dropping below 5 °C at low load, so it cannot freeze |
| Pressure gauges and switches | High and low pressure protection for the refrigeration circuit |

## Air-cooled or water-cooled?

An **air-cooled** chiller rejects heat through finned condensers and fans, so it needs only power and fresh air. A **water-cooled** chiller rejects heat into water from a [cooling tower](/products/round-cooling-towers.html); it suits plants that already run a tower. WCP chillers are offered with either.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the main parts of a process chiller?
A brazed plate heat exchanger (evaporator), refrigeration compressor, condenser and fans, circulating pump, insulated tank, temperature controller, hot gas bypass valve, and pressure gauges and switches.

### What is the difference between an air-cooled and a water-cooled chiller?
An air-cooled chiller rejects heat through finned condensers and fans and needs only power and fresh air. A water-cooled chiller rejects heat into water from a cooling tower and suits plants that already run a tower.

### What does the hot gas bypass valve do in a chiller?
It stops the evaporator dropping below 5 °C at low load, so the water in it cannot freeze.

### Why does a chiller have a closed stainless steel tank?
A closed, insulated tank stops the chilled water gaining heat from the room before it is pumped back to the process.
