An air compressor without a dryer delivers water along with the air. A 100 CFM compressor working continuously can put around 34 litres of water a day into the system (calculated for saturated air at 45 °C and 7 bar g, dried to +3 °C). Here is what removing it does for a plant.

## 1. Pipes and receivers stop corroding

Liquid water in steel pipework and receivers causes rust and scale. Scale breaks off and travels downstream into valves and filters, and corroded pipes leak. Dry air at a +3 °C pressure dew point cannot condense in pipework that stays above +3 °C.

## 2. Valves, cylinders and tools last longer

Water washes lubricant out of pneumatic valves, cylinders and air tools and causes corrosion inside them. Keeping liquid water out of the lines protects these components.

## 3. Product quality stays consistent

Where air touches the product, water shows up as defects: spots in paint finishes, marks on fabric, wet blow-off on parts, moisture in packaging. A dryer removes the source.

## 4. Less maintenance on the rest of the air system

Downstream filters load up faster with water and rust. With dry air they reach their 0.7 bar g change point less often, and drains, regulators and lubricators have less to deal with.

## 5. Small running cost, small pressure loss

A refrigerated dryer needs no purge air. Its running cost is the refrigeration compressor and fan. WRD dryers are rated for a pressure drop of 0.2 bar g or less, so the compressor does not need to run at a noticeably higher pressure to make up for it.

## What a refrigerated dryer does not do

It does not reach a dew point below freezing (for that you need a [desiccant dryer](/products/desiccant-air-dryers.html)), and it does not remove oil aerosols or fine particles on its own (for that you need [filters](/products/compressed-air-filters.html)). Most plants combine a dryer with a pre-filter and, where air quality matters, finer filters after it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much water does a compressor put into the air system?
A 100 CFM compressor working continuously can put around 34 litres of water a day into the system (calculated for saturated air at 45 °C and 7 bar g, dried to +3 °C).

### Does a refrigerated air dryer use purge air?
No. Its running cost is the refrigeration compressor and fan. Desiccant dryers are the type that use purge air.

### Can a refrigerated dryer reach a dew point below freezing?
No. For a dew point below freezing you need a desiccant dryer.

### Does a refrigerated air dryer remove oil?
Not on its own. Oil aerosols and fine particles need compressed air filters. Most plants combine a dryer with a pre-filter and, where air quality matters, finer filters after it.
