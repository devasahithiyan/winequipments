Most of our chillers are standard WCP water chillers that serve several industries. A few duties need their own design. This guide maps each industry to the right chiller, using the application lists in our catalogue.

## Plastics and injection moulding

Injection moulding and plastic machines are the most common use of our air-cooled and water-cooled WCP chillers. The [air-cooled chillers](/products/air-cooled-chillers.html) suit moulding shops without a cooling tower or with poor water supply. The [industrial process chillers](/products/industrial-process-chillers.html) cover larger or water-cooled duties.

## Hydraulic presses, oil and lubricant cooling

Hydraulic presses and drives, and lubricant and oil cooling, use the WCP process chillers and the air-cooled chillers. Size the chiller from the oil flow and the temperature drop the oil must achieve; the [sizing guide](/blog/how-to-size-a-process-chiller.html) shows the sum.

## Compressor cooling

Compressor cooling appears in both the air-cooled and the WCP process chiller applications. The chiller rejects the compressor heat to water or air, so check the ambient temperature in the sizing step.

## Food, dairy and beverage

- **Milk, dairy and pasteurising, and food products:** WCP process chillers and air-cooled chillers. For milk and coconut water processing we build [milk chiller plants](/products/milk-chillers.html) to order.
- **Soda and carbonated drinks, beverage filling lines, chilled drinking water:** [soda chillers](/products/soda-chillers.html), engineered for beverage plants.

## Automobile, pulp and paper, printing, sugar and footwear

Our process chiller applications also list automobile plants, pulp and paper, printing ink and paper waxing, sugar, and footwear. These use the standard WCP range, sized from the duty.

## Acid, anodizing and medical scan

- **Acid cooling:** [acid cooling chillers](/products/acid-cooling-chillers.html), engineered to order.
- **Anodizing and electroplating:** [anodizing chillers](/products/anodizing-chillers.html), engineered to order.
- **MRI and CT scan:** [medical scan chillers](/products/medical-scan-chillers.html), engineered to order.

## Not sure which one?

Send us your process, the water flow and temperatures, and the site ambient. We will recommend a chiller and the cooling type. Use the [how to choose a chiller guide](/blog/how-to-choose-a-chiller.html) for the checklist.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which chiller suits an injection moulding shop?
An air-cooled chiller suits a moulding shop with no cooling tower or poor water supply. A WCP process chiller covers larger or water-cooled duties. Send the water flow and the temperature the mould needs, with the site ambient, for a sized model.

### Do you make chillers for milk and dairy?
Yes. Our milk and coconut water chillers are built to order. Send the volume and the temperatures you need.
