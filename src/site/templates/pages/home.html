What a refrigerated dryer does

Air leaves the compressor saturated with water. A WRD dryer chills it to a +3 °C pressure dew point so the water condenses and drains out before it reaches your machines.

  1. Pre-cool

    Outgoing cold air pre-cools the hot, wet air coming in.

  2. Chill to +3 °C

    The evaporator chills the air to the dew point; the vapour condenses.

  3. Drain

    The separator and automatic drain remove the water.
