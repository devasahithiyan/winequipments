Manufacturer, supplier and project partner

Win Equipments was established in {{ site.founded }} as a manufacturer of compressed air and water cooling systems. We make the equipment, and we also take on project consultancy: designing, planning and commissioning the system around it.

Our equipment is fabricated and assembled at our works in Arasur by the team pictured above. After commissioning we support each installation with service and spares.

  • Compressed air treatment: refrigerated and desiccant dryers, filters, drain valves, air receivers and aftercoolers
  • Process cooling: WCP process chillers and chillers engineered for anodizing, acid, medical scan and ice flake duties
  • Heat rejection: round and square FRP cooling towers and coil cooling towers
