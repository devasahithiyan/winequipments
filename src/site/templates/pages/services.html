When to call us

Signs your equipment needs a service

What operators usually notice first, and what it most often points to.

  • ChillerTrips on high pressure

    Usually a blocked or dirty condenser, or a failed condenser fan.

  • ChillerWater not reaching set temperature

    Condenser fouling, a refrigerant problem, or a heat load above the chiller's rating.

  • Cooling towerOutlet water too warm

    Scaled or broken fills, blocked nozzles or a slow fan: the tower no longer reaches its design outlet temperature.

  • Cooling towerWater channelling or drifting out

    Water running through in streams points to damaged fills; spray carried out with the air points to drift eliminators.

  • Air dryerWater downstream of the dryer

    A drain not discharging, an open bypass valve, or a dryer overloaded for the actual air flow.

  • Air dryerHigh dew point or frosting

    A dirty condenser, hot inlet air, or a hot gas bypass valve that needs adjustment.
