About Win Equipments

Pioneering precision compressed air treatment & process thermal systems in Coimbatore, India

Founded in 2008 by industrial engineering veteran {{ company.founder }}, Win Equipments has grown from an innovative design workshop into one of South India's foremost manufacturers of high-efficiency industrial compressed air dryers, process chillers, cooling towers, and heavy pressure vessels.

Operating from its integrated manufacturing plant at SF No: 4, 195 B, Kallangadu, Arasur Post, Coimbatore, the company upholds a foundational commitment encapsulated in its guiding motto: "Save Water and Power". Every machine is engineered specifically to withstand severe Indian tropical and Middle-Eastern ambient conditions (up to 45°C continuous and 50°C peak), delivering unyielding reliability where generic machinery fails.

Win Equipments Manufacturing Plant, Arasur Works, Coimbatore
🔬 Integrated Design & Fabrication

In-house CNC machining, automated submerged-arc welding (SAW), high-precision sheet metal shearing, and electrostatic powder coating bays ensure total quality control from raw steel plates to final crated skids.

🌡 Extreme Tropical Ambient Duty

Unlike European and imported machinery that trips on high head pressure during Indian summer heat peaks, Win Equipments oversized condenser coils and generous heat exchanger areas deliver full rated capacity at 45°C ambient.

⚡ Thermodynamic Energy Conservation

From zero-air-loss capacitive drains saving ₹42,000/yr per compressor, to 3-in-1 modular regenerator heat exchangers reducing refrigeration kW by 40%, our systems are engineered to minimize client utility bills.

🛠 Direct Factory Engineering Support

Over 1,200 active installations across India backed by direct Coimbatore factory field engineers, fully stocked OEM spare parts warehousing, and comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).

Manufacturing Infrastructure & Rigorous Quality Testing

Accredited quality management systems conforming to ISO 9001:2015, IAF & DAC standards
✓ 4-Stage Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)
  • Hydrostatic Proof Testing: 1.5× design pressure hold test on all pressure vessels, coils, and buffer tanks.
  • Helium Leak Detection: Mass spectrometer vacuum testing of refrigeration circuits (<0.1 g/year loss).
  • Thermal Calorimeter Testing: 8-hour continuous full-load simulation verifying kW heat removal and dew point.
  • Electrical Safety & Megger Testing: High-potential dielectric breakdown and earthing bonding audit.
★ Global Accreditation Badges
  • ISO 9001:2015: Certified Quality Management System covering design, manufacturing, testing, and servicing.
  • IAF (International Accreditation Forum): Globally recognized conformity assessment and trade compliance.
  • DAC (Dubai Accreditation Center): Certified for extreme desert high-ambient installations across GCC regions.
  • Boiler Regulations: Fabrication compliance with IS 2825 Class II and ASME Section VIII Division 1.
Zero-Defect Quality Policy

Every system manufactured at Arasur Works undergoes individualized FAT protocol testing. Test data, refrigerant charge records, electrical logs, and welder qualification credentials are permanently archived and supplied with each machine's documentation package.
