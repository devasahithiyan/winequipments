How It Works

Working Principle

01

BPHE Efficiency

The Heat Exchanger is of BPHE type, chosen to maintain fluid velocities that promote turbulence and maximize heat transfer.

02

Counter Flow

A Counter Flow pattern is utilized to achieve the maximum temperature difference with minimum pressure drop.

03

Refrigeration Cycle

The system uses a compressor, condenser, and expansion device to absorb heat from the water in the evaporator.

04

Circulation

Cooled water is pumped out of the Chiller through a dedicated pump provided at the outlet.

How to Select the Right Chiller

Method: You only need two key data points to select the right re-circulating Industrial Chiller.

Step 1

Determine the required Outlet Temperature (cold temp) of water in °C / °F.

Step 2

Calculate the Heat Load (Q) to be removed.

Q = m x ΔT

  • m = Mass flow rate (lpm)
  • ΔT = Inlet Temp - Outlet Temp
  • Q (Kcal/hr) = (Kcal/min) x 60

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What temperature range can your chillers achieve?

Our standard industrial chillers offer a temperature range of 5°C to 30°C. For specific low-temperature applications (like glycol chillers), we can design systems for sub-zero temperatures.

Do you offer both air-cooled and water-cooled models?

Yes, we manufacture both. Air-cooled chillers are easier to install and require less maintenance, while water-cooled chillers are more efficient for larger capacities (above 20 TR).

Is this suitable for plastic injection molding?

Absolutely. Our chillers are widely used to cool molds in injection molding machines, reducing cycle time and improving product finish.

What safety protections are included?

Our chillers come with high/low pressure switches, antifreeze thermostats, overload protection for compressors/pumps, and flow switches to ensure safe and reliable operation.
