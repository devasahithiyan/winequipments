Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which type of drain valve should I choose?

Select Timer-based for general use, Float-based for sites without power, and Zero Air Loss for maximum energy savings.

What is the maximum pressure rating?

Our high-pressure models can withstand up to 70 bar g, making them suitable for PET bottling and other high-pressure applications.

Does it require electricity?

Timer-based and Zero Air Loss models require 230V AC (or optional DC), while the Mechanical Float type operates without any power.

How do I prevent clogging?

Our valves feature a large orifice and some models come with a built-in strainer/ball valve to prevent debris from clogging the discharge.
