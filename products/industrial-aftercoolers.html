What does a compressed air aftercooler do? An aftercooler is a specialized heat exchanger installed immediately after an air compressor discharge port. As air is compressed to 7 to 10 bar, compression heat raises its temperature to between 100°C and 150°C. The aftercooler uses either cooling water or forced ambient air to cool the air stream down to 35°C–40°C. This rapid temperature drop condenses up to 75% of the air's moisture into liquid water, which is captured and drained before the air enters the factory distribution piping or air dryer.
