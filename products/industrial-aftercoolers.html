Technical FAQ

Compressed Air Aftercooler FAQs

Answers regarding sizing, cooling medium selection, and maintenance.

Why is an external aftercooler needed if my compressor has an internal radiator?

During summer months in industrial corridors like Coimbatore and Chennai, compressor canopy temperatures exceed 45°C. Internal coolers often fail to bring discharge air below 55°C–65°C, which causes downstream air dryers to trip on high thermal overload. An external aftercooler drops air to within 5°C–10°C of ambient.

What is the difference between air-cooled and water-cooled aftercoolers?

Air-cooled models use copper tube / aluminum fin coils with electric fans—ideal for standalone installations with zero water piping. Water-cooled models utilize shell-and-tube heat exchangers with cooling tower water, offering compact footprints and consistent cooling in hot ambient zones.
