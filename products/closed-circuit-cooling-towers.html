What is a closed circuit cooling tower? A closed circuit cooling tower (also known as an evaporative fluid cooler) isolates the primary process cooling water inside a closed loop of copper or stainless steel tubing coils. A secondary spray water system cascades over the outside of the coils while air is drawn upward by axial fans. This provides all the thermal heat-rejection benefits of evaporative cooling without ever allowing the process fluid to contact atmospheric dust, dirt, or bacteria, permanently preventing scaling inside induction furnace coils and laser cooling jackets.
