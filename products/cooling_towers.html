How It Works

Working Principle

01

Water Distribution

Hot process water is sprayed evenly over the high-efficiency PVC fills.

02

Air-Water Contact

Ambient air is drawn up through the fills, maximizing contact surface.

03

Evaporative Cooling

A small portion of water evaporates, removing heat from the remaining water.

04

Cold Water Basin

Cooled water collects at the bottom, ready for recirculation.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What fill material do you use?

We use high-efficiency PVC honeycomb fills. These provide maximum surface area for air-water contact, ensuring superior heat rejection compared to traditional splash fills.

How often should I clean the basin?

We recommend cleaning the cold water basin every 3 months to prevent algae growth and scale buildup. Our towers come with an easy-access drain valve for quick maintenance.

Is the casing rust-proof?

Yes, our cooling towers are made of high-grade Fiber Reinforced Plastic (FRP), which is completely corrosion-resistant and UV-stabilized for long outdoor life.

What is the water loss (drift loss)?

Our aerodynamic design and efficient drift eliminators reduce water loss to less than 0.05%, making our towers highly water-efficient.
