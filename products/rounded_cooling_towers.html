How It Works

Working Principle

01

Hot Water Inlet

Process water enters the tower and is distributed through a spray system.

02

Heat Exchange

Water flows down through honeycomb PVC fills while air is drawn upwards.

03

Evaporation

The counter-flow design maximizes evaporation, rapidly cooling the water.

04

Cold Water Sump

Cooled water gathers in the basin for recirculation to the plant.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main advantage of a Square Cooling Tower?

Square cooling towers are modular and space-saving. They can be installed side-by-side to increase capacity without wasting floor space, unlike round bottle towers.

Are they quieter than round towers?

Yes, generally square towers have better airflow dynamics and can be equipped with low-noise fans, making them suitable for hospitals and residential areas.

Can I expand the capacity later?

Yes, due to their modular design, you can easily add another cell adjacent to the existing tower to double the capacity as your plant grows.

Is the maintenance difficult?

Not at all. Our square towers feature large inspection doors and walkways for easy internal access to fills and drift eliminators for cleaning.
