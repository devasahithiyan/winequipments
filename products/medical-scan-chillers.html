Mission Critical Uptime

Zero-Downtime Protection for Multi-Crore Imaging Systems

A failure in MRI cold head cooling triggers rapid helium boil-off, costing hospitals ₹25 to ₹50 Lakhs in gas refilling and days of cancelled patient appointments. Win medical scan chillers integrate four fail-safe security layers:

True N+1 Dual Circuit Redundancy

Equipped with two completely isolated refrigeration systems (compressor, condenser, evaporator, and expansion valve). In the event of a fault on Circuit 1, Circuit 2 starts automatically within milliseconds to sustain continuous cooling.

Automated City Water Bypass

Spring-return motorized three-way bypass valve connected to facility domestic water line. If building power is lost or both refrigeration circuits lock out, cool city water flushes through the scanner heat exchanger, saving the helium cold head.

Dual SS316 Pumps with Auto-Switch

Twin stainless steel centrifugal pumps operating in lead/lag configuration. The PLC alternates pump duty cycles every 24 hours to balance wear and triggers immediate instant cutover if flow switch detects pressure drop.

Hospital Engineering FAQ

Medical Scan Chiller Technical Questions

How does the automated emergency city water bypass prevent helium quench?

MRI scanners require uninterrupted cooling to prevent liquid helium from warming above its boiling threshold. If both chiller circuits trip or if utility power is disconnected, a spring-return motorized 3-way valve triggers instantly, opening the hospital's treated water line through the MRI compressor. This preserves the cold head shield until power or maintenance is restored.

Why are non-ferrous SS316 hydronic components mandatory for diagnostic imaging?

Ordinary carbon steel or cast iron piping sheds microscopic rust particles over time. These particles foul the delicate micro-heat exchangers inside medical imaging gantries, causing expensive premature scanner component failure. Win Equipments uses 100% SS304/SS316 wetted parts to maintain pristine fluid purity.

What sound attenuation measures are included for hospital installations?

Hospital imaging suites have strict acoustic limits. Our chillers feature acoustic compressor jackets, neoprene vibration isolators, sickle-blade low-RPM condenser fans, and optional sound-baffled air discharge plenums to maintain noise levels below 65 dBA.

Can Win Equipments interface with hospital Building Management Systems (BMS)?

Yes. Every Win medical chiller includes RS-485 Modbus RTU communication and dry potential-free contacts for common fault, low-flow warning, high-temperature alarm, and pump run status, allowing hospital maintenance teams to monitor chiller health in real time.
