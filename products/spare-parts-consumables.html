Desiccant Media PART: WE-AA-400

Activated Alumina Spheres (AA-400)

High crush-strength, low-dusting spherical activated alumina for heatless and heated desiccant air dryers. Delivers reliable -40°C pressure dew point with superior surface area adsorption (>340 m²/g).

  • Available Sizes: 3–5 mm & 4–8 mm bead diameters
  • Crush Strength: >160 N per sphere (prevents tower dusting)
  • Packaging: 25 kg / 50 kg moisture-proof HDPE lined bags
  • Compatibility: Win, Trident, Delair, Sanpar desiccant towers

Deep Cryo Media PART: WE-MS-13X

Molecular Sieve Type 13X / 4A

Synthetic zeolite with precise 10Å aperture size engineered for extreme dew point suppression down to -70°C PDP. Essential for medical air, electronics cleanrooms, and cryogenic oxygen systems.

  • Available Types: Type 13X (beads 1.6–2.5 mm), Type 4A (2.0–3.0 mm)
  • Water Adsorption Capacity: >21.5% wt at 25°C, 50% RH
  • Bulk Density: 0.68–0.72 g/ml
  • Packaging: Hermetically sealed 25 kg steel drums

0.01μ Borosilicate Coalescing Cartridges

Multi-layer borosilicate glass microfiber filter elements with perforated SS304 inner and outer retainers. Coalesces aerosol mist and removes particulate down to 0.01 micron with residual oil <0.01 ppm.

  • End Caps: Glass-filled nylon with double Viton O-ring seals

Frequently Asked Spares Questions

Guidance on ordering, compatibility verification, and same-day dispatch.

Are Win Equipments replacement filters and desiccant compatible with other brands?

Yes. Our activated alumina, molecular sieves, and coalescing filter cartridges conform to standard dimensional and ISO 8573-1 air quality standards, making them drop-in replacements for Trident, Delair, Sanpar, Ultrafilter, and Domnick Hunter units.

How do I identify the right part if my machine nameplate is missing?

Simply take a photograph of your existing damaged part, filter element end-cap, or pipe connection and send it via WhatsApp to +91 95972 28969. Our technical engineering desk will measure and verify the exact match within 30 minutes.

How fast are replacement spares dispatched?

Standard filter cartridges, zero air loss drains, and activated alumina are in ready stock at our Arasur, Coimbatore warehouse for same-day dispatch via express courier or direct counter pickup for emergency breakdown repairs.
