How It Works

Working Principle

01

Dual Tower Design

Two vessels filled with desiccant ensure continuous operation.

02

Adsorption Phase

Wet air passes through Tower A, where moisture is trapped by the desiccant.

03

Regeneration Phase

Tower B is depressurized and purged to remove trapped moisture.

04

Cycle Switching

The system automatically switches towers to maintain a steady flow of dry air.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What dew point can this dryer achieve?

Our Desiccant Air Dryers consistently achieve a pressure dew point of -40°C, ensuring zero moisture in your compressed air lines.

How often do I need to change the desiccant?

With proper maintenance and pre-filtration, the Activated Alumina desiccant typically lasts between 2 to 3 years.

Do I need filters with this dryer?

Yes, a pre-filter is required to protect the desiccant from oil, and a post-filter is needed to prevent desiccant dust from entering the air stream.

Is it suitable for medical applications?

Yes, due to the ultra-low dew point, these dryers are ideal for hospitals, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and food packaging.
