What is a square cooling tower? A square cooling tower is an evaporative heat rejection system configured with a rectangular or square footprint, utilizing honeycomb PVC film fills and an induced draft axial fan. Because square towers can be installed side-by-side in multi-cell banks, they save up to 40% valuable plant floor space compared to round bottle towers, making them the preferred cooling choice for plastic molding factories, HVAC plants, and chemical processing units.
