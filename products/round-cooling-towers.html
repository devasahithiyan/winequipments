Technical FAQ

Round Bottle Cooling Tower Engineering Questions

What makes round bottle cooling towers aerodynamically efficient?

The circular bottle shape eliminates dead air zones and reduces air resistance. Uniform 360-degree air intake around the base provides balanced contact with falling water droplets, minimizing fan motor power requirements.

What is the expected lifespan of Win Equipments FRP cooling towers?

Manufactured with premium isopthalic resin and UV-resistant gel coats, our FRP cooling towers deliver 15 to 20+ years of operational life without corrosion, even when handling aggressive water or outdoor tropical weathering.
