What is a round bottle cooling tower? A round bottle cooling tower is a vertical induced-draft cooling system designed with a circular FRP casing. Hot process water is sprayed across circular PVC honeycomb fills via a self-rotating sprinkler head, while ambient air enters evenly through a 360-degree louvered perimeter at the base. This circular counterflow path eliminates air dead spots, lowers fan motor power consumption, and provides the highest cooling efficiency per rupee of capital investment for diesel generators, compressors, and plastic injection machines.

Technical FAQ

Round Bottle Cooling Tower Engineering Questions

What makes round bottle cooling towers aerodynamically efficient?

The circular bottle shape eliminates dead air zones and reduces air resistance. Uniform 360-degree air intake around the base provides balanced contact with falling water droplets, minimizing fan motor power requirements.
