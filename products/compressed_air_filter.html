Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between filter grades?

Grades indicate filtration capability. GP is for coarse particles (3 micron), ZP for fine oil/particles (0.1 micron), and ZC (Activated Carbon) is for removing oil vapors and odors.

When should I replace the filter element?

We recommend replacing elements every 2000 operating hours or when the differential pressure indicator turns red (usually above 0.5 bar pressure drop).

Can these filters remove oil?

Yes, our ZP and ZO grade coalescing filters are specifically designed to remove oil aerosols down to 0.01 mg/m³.

Do they meet ISO standards?

Yes, our filters are tested and validated to meet ISO 8573-1 air quality standards for solid particulates, moisture, and oil.
