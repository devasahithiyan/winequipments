What is an industrial process chiller? An industrial process chiller is a centralized cooling system that circulates chilled fluid (water, glycol, or oil) at a strictly maintained temperature to remove process heat from industrial machinery. Featuring hermetic scroll compressors, high-efficiency heat exchangers, and PID microcontrollers, process chillers reduce cycle times in plastic injection molding, protect fiber laser optical heads, and prevent thermal growth in CNC machine spindles.

Typical Closed-Loop Industrial Chilling Circuit

Integrated internal SS304 buffer reservoir and centrifugal pump system designed for continuous 24/7 duty without compressor short-cycling.

1. Thermal Buffer Mass: The oversized SS304 internal tank absorbs sudden load swings from molding machines or laser piercing, preventing compressor hunting.
2. Stainless Steel Sanitary Circuit: Wetted parts (tank, evaporator coil, and centrifugal pump impeller) are non-ferrous SS304/SS316, preventing rust from clogging micro-cooling channels.
3. Precise Microprocessor Control: Digital PID controller dynamically modulates hot-gas bypass to maintain precision water temperature within ±0.5°C of setpoint.

Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions: Industrial Process Chillers

What is the difference between air-cooled and water-cooled process chillers?

Air-cooled chillers use finned air condensers and axial fans to reject heat directly into ambient air, requiring zero water consumption and simpler installation. Water-cooled chillers reject heat into a cooling tower water loop, offering higher thermodynamic efficiency for large capacities above 20 TR.

Can Win Equipments chillers provide dual-temperature circuits for fiber laser cutters?

Yes. We engineer specialized dual-circuit laser chillers with independent temperature loops: Circuit 1 maintains 22°C (±0.5°C) for the fiber laser resonator, while Circuit 2 maintains 28°C (±1°C) for the laser cutting optics head to eliminate condensation fogging.

How does the internal SS304 buffer tank prevent compressor short-cycling?

Our chillers feature an oversized insulated stainless steel reservoir that absorbs sudden batch thermal load swings from plastic injection cycles or CNC spindle ramps. This prevents frequent compressor on/off cycling, dramatically extending compressor lifespan and reducing peak electrical demand.
