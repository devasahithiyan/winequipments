How It Works

Working Principle

Compressed air is the most powerful & useful, portable, easy to use economical source of energy. In manufacturing processess, almost at all stages the compressed air is required and 30% of electrical energy is used for conversion of pnematic energy, the compressed air.

Air contains contaminants like solid (dust particles) liquid (moisture) an oil (few traces), which contaminates the compressed air. this untreated wet compressed air enters into the equipments causing failures and affects the production process. Thus, compressed air is to be treated to make it Dry Air, free from contaminants.

Saturated compressed air enters the Pre-cooler / Re-heater, where it is pre-cooled by exchanging heat with the outgoing cold air, which reduces heat load to evaporator. The inlet air is further cooled in the evaporator to the required dew point. The cold air is then reheated when it passes back through the air-to-air heat exchanger. A refrigerant compressor and condenser supply low-temperature refrigerant to the evaporator. The thermostatic expansion valve and the hot gas bypass valve match the operation of the refrigerant system to the compressed air cooling load. The process cools the air, reducing its capacity to hold water vapor and resulting in moisture condensation.

01

Air Intake

Hot, moist compressed air enters the dryer and passes through an air-to-air heat exchanger.

02

Cooling

The air is cooled to a precise temperature (+3°C) by the refrigeration circuit, causing moisture to condense.

03

Separation

Condensed water droplets are removed from the air stream by a high-efficiency separator and automatically drained.

04

Discharge

Dry, cool air is reheated by the incoming air, preventing pipe sweating and increasing system efficiency.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the warranty period for your air dryers?

We provide a standard 12-month warranty on all our refrigerated air dryers. The compressor comes with an extended warranty depending on the model (Emerson/Danfoss). We also offer AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts) for post-warranty support.

Do I need a pre-filter installed before the dryer?

Yes, we highly recommend installing a pre-filter (5 micron or better) to remove bulk dust and oil aerosols before the air enters the dryer. This protects the heat exchanger from fouling and maintains efficiency.

How do I choose the right capacity (CFM)?

Select a dryer with a capacity at least equal to your compressor's output (CFM). However, you must apply "Correction Factors" for inlet temperature and working pressure. Please refer to our Selection Chart in the Specs tab or contact our engineers for sizing.

Is installation support available in Coimbatore/Tamil Nadu?

Absolutely. For customers in Coimbatore and Tamil Nadu, our service team handles commissioning and on-site training. For other locations, we provide detailed video guides and remote support.

What refrigerant do you use?

We use R134a and R407c, which are eco-friendly, non-ozone depleting refrigerants compliant with international environmental standards.
