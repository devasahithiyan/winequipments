How It Works

Working Principle

01

Fluid Circulation

Hot process fluid circulates through high-efficiency copper coils.

02

Forced Airflow

Axial fans pull ambient air across the coil surface.

03

Heat Exchange

Heat is transferred from the fluid to the air without direct contact.

04

Cooled Return

The cooled fluid is recirculated back to the equipment.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the advantage of a closed-loop system?

Closed-loop systems prevent process fluid contamination, reduce scaling and fouling, and minimize water loss by keeping the fluid isolated from the atmosphere.

What material is used for the coils?

We strictly use high-grade Copper tubes for superior heat transfer efficiency and corrosion resistance, ensuring a long operational life.

Can this handle high inlet temperatures?

Yes, our coil cooling towers are designed to handle high inlet temperatures up to 90°C efficiently.

How often does it need maintenance?

Due to the closed-loop design, maintenance is minimal compared to open towers. Periodic checking of fan motors and coil cleanliness is recommended.
