Chemical Compatibility Engineering

Tailored Metallurgy for Severe Corrosive Media

Selecting incorrect materials in acid chilling leads to catastrophic coil failure, chemical contamination, and explosive gas emissions. Win Equipments matches metallurgy precisely to acid chemistry:

Grade 2 Pure Titanium

Best for: Sulfuric Acid (<25%), Nitric Acid, Chromic Acid, and Anodizing Baths. Titanium forms an instantaneous self-healing oxide film that makes it 100% resistant to oxidizing acid attack up to 60°C.

Hastelloy C-276 Superalloy

Best for: Hydrochloric Acid (HCl), Chlorinated Solvents, and Ferric Chloride Pickling. High nickel-molybdenum-chromium content provides exceptional resistance to severe chloride pitting and stress corrosion cracking.

Teflon (PTFE / PFA) Bundles

Best for: Mixed Pickling Acids containing Hydrofluoric Acid (HF + HNO₃) or aggressive chemical reaction baths. Fluoropolymer tubes are totally chemically inert to all industrial acids across extreme pH ranges.

Chemical FAQ

Acid Cooling Chiller Engineering Questions

When should I use Pure Titanium vs Hastelloy C-276 for acid cooling?

Pure Titanium Grade 2 is the most cost-effective and durable solution for Sulfuric Acid (up to 20–25%) and Nitric Acid environments. However, in Hydrochloric Acid (HCl), titanium can suffer rapid degradation due to hydrogen absorption and lack of oxidizing agents. For HCl or mixed solutions with active fluorides, Hastelloy C-276 or Teflon PTFE exchangers are required.

Why are magnetic drive pumps superior to mechanical seal pumps in acid chillers?

Mechanical shaft seals have rotating faces that wear out, causing aggressive acids to spray or seep onto plant floors and corrode motor shafts. Sealless magnetic drive pumps utilize a magnetic field across a hermetically sealed containment shell, completely eliminating dynamic seals and offering 100% leak-free containment.

Can Win acid chillers handle direct acid circulation through the evaporator?

Yes. Depending on your preference, we can circulate the acid directly through our Titanium / Hastelloy Plate Heat Exchanger using PVDF magnetic pumps, or we can circulate chilled water from the chiller into a Grade 2 Titanium or Teflon drop-in immersion coil situated inside your acid tank.

How are electrical panels protected against corrosive acid fumes in plating halls?

Acid vapors quickly destroy contactors, microprocessors, and copper busbars. Win acid chillers feature sealed IP65 enclosures with neoprene continuous gaskets, positive cabinet air pressurization options, and conformal-coated printed circuit boards to ensure trouble-free service in acidic plant ambients.
