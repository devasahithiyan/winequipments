Engineering Sizing Rule

How to Size Anodizing Chiller Tonnage from Rectifier Power

In aluminum anodizing, nearly 98% of the electrical energy supplied by the DC rectifier transforms directly into thermal dissipation inside the acid bath. In addition, exothermic chemical oxide formation generates roughly 1,600 BTU per pound of aluminum dissolved.

Rectifier Heat Load (kW) = (Volts × Amperes) / 1,000
Required Tonnage (TR) = (Volts × Amps × 3.412) / 12,000 × 1.15

Practical Shop Example

A commercial architectural aluminum profile plant running a 18V, 3,000 Ampere anodizing rectifier:

  • Total Power: 18 V × 3,000 A = 54,000 Watts (54 kW)
  • Heat Equivalent: 54,000 × 3.412 = 184,248 BTU/hr
  • Base Tonnage: 184,248 / 12,000 = 15.35 TR
  • Recommended Chiller with 15% safety factor = WAN 175 (17.5 TR) or WAN 200 (20 TR)

Technical FAQ

Anodizing Chiller Engineering Questions

How is chiller tonnage sized from rectifier amperage and voltage?

Because over 95% of DC electrical power turns to heat in the bath, we calculate heat load in BTU/hr by multiplying Watts (Volts × Amperes) by 3.412, then dividing by 12,000 BTU/TR. We then add a 15% buffer for tank ambient absorption and chemical reaction enthalpy: Tonnage = (V × A × 3.412 / 12,000) × 1.15.

Why must anodizing evaporators use Pure Titanium Grade 2 instead of Stainless Steel?

Sulfuric acid baths rapidly cause pinhole pitting in SS304 and even SS316 under high DC voltage fields. Grade 2 Titanium is naturally self-passivating in sulfuric acid up to 20% concentration, ensuring lifelong zero-leak reliability and no contamination of costly chemical baths.

What is the advantage of direct tank titanium drop-in coils versus external PHE?

Direct drop-in coils submerge directly into the anodizing tank along the side walls. Chilled water (or refrigerant) circulates inside the titanium tubes. This eliminates the need for expensive magnetic acid pumps and guarantees zero acid leaks onto factory floors. External Titanium Plate Heat Exchangers (PHE) are preferred for high volume automated lines where tank space is constrained.

Can Win Equipments supply hard coat anodizing chillers capable of sub-zero operation?

Yes. For Type III Hard Anodizing operating at -2°C to +4°C, we provide chillers engineered for non-freezing propylene glycol/water brine with low-temperature Copeland scroll or Bitzer semi-hermetic compressors and dual crankcase heaters.
