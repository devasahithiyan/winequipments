What is an automatic drain valve for an air compressor? An automatic drain valve is a specialized solenoid or capacitive valve installed at the lowest drainage points of air receiver tanks, refrigerated dryers, and moisture separators. It automatically discharges accumulated condensate water and oily sludge without requiring manual operator intervention. High-efficiency zero air loss drains discharge only liquid while sealing shut before compressed air escapes, saving thousands of kilowatt-hours in compressor electrical power each year.

Technical FAQ

Automatic Drain Valve FAQs

Key differences between electronic timer and zero-loss capacitive drains.

How does a capacitive zero air loss drain save electrical power?

Traditional timer solenoid valves open every few minutes regardless of water level, venting expensive compressed air (costing ₹12,000–₹25,000 per year per valve). Zero air loss drains utilize an electronic capacitive sensor that only opens when liquid is detected and closes before air escapes, saving 100% of wasted air.

Can the valve handle oily compressor sludge and scale?

Yes. Win Equipments drain valves feature a heavy-duty forged brass body with a stainless steel integrated mesh strainer and direct-acting discharge orifice that resists sludge clogging. A manual push-to-test button allows instant line flushing.

What voltage and connection sizes are standard?

Standard models run on single-phase 230V AC (50Hz) with IP65 weather-sealed enclosures. Condensate inlets are standard 1/2" BSP female threads with 1/4" or 1/2" outlet hose barb connections.
