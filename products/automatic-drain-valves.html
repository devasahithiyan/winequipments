What is an automatic drain valve for an air compressor? An automatic drain valve is a specialized solenoid or capacitive valve installed at the lowest drainage points of air receiver tanks, refrigerated dryers, and moisture separators. It automatically discharges accumulated condensate water and oily sludge without requiring manual operator intervention. High-efficiency zero air loss drains discharge only liquid while sealing shut before compressed air escapes, saving thousands of kilowatt-hours in compressor electrical power each year.
