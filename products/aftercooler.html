How It Works

Working Principle

01

Hot Air Inlet

High-temperature compressed air enters the heat exchanger.

02

Rapid Cooling

Ambient air or water circulates around the tubes, absorbing heat.

03

Condensation

As the temperature drops, water vapor turns into liquid condensate.

04

Moisture Separation

The liquid is separated and drained, leaving cooler, drier air.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it reduce the air temperature?

Our aftercoolers are designed to reduce the compressed air temperature from around 120°C to within 10°C of the ambient temperature (approx. 40°C).

Does it remove moisture completely?

It removes about 70-75% of the moisture by condensation. For complete moisture removal, a refrigerated or desiccant dryer should be installed downstream.

Should I choose air-cooled or water-cooled?

Air-cooled is simpler and requires no water supply. Water-cooled is more efficient and better suited for hot environments or engine rooms with poor ventilation.

What is the pressure drop across the cooler?

Our heat exchangers are designed for minimal flow resistance, ensuring a pressure drop of less than 0.2 bar.
