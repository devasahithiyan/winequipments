Refrigerated Air Dryers (Compressed Air Dryer)

Original:

Win Equipments offers state-of-the-art Refrigeration Air Dryers that efficiently remove moisture from compressed air. Combining advanced refrigeration technology... (implied)

Improved:
Our Refrigerated Air Dryers use rugged refrigeration cycles to extract moisture from compressed air, ensuring a steady dew point and protecting downstream equipment. Built with heavy-duty heat exchangers (coaxial or shell-tube coils) and efficient reciprocating/scroll compressors, these dryers condense out up to 99% of water vapor under load. Key features include automatic condensate drains (with zero-loss timers), digital temperature/dew-point controls, and hot-gas bypass valves for continuous operation. By lowering the air temperature, they prevent corrosion, freeze-ups and quality issues in pneumatic tools and controls. Our dryers are available in air‑cooled or water‑cooled versions and capacities from small industrial (e.g. 20–50 CFM) up to large plant-scale units. They are engineered for low energy consumption and easy maintenance (accessible filters and gauges). Applications: Ideal for factories and plants that require clean, dry air – for example automotive workshops, food and beverage production, textile manufacturing or chemical processing – our dryers ensure reliable air quality while cutting operational costs and conserving power.

winequipments.com

An Air Receiver is a vital component in any compressed air system. It stores the compressed air produced by your compressor, dampens pressure pulsations, and maintains a stable supply for downstream applications.
winequipments.com

Improved:
Win’s Air Receivers act as a buffer and surge tank for compressed air systems, providing large storage capacity and smoothing out pressure fluctuations. Constructed from thick, high-grade steel, each receiver tank is pressure-tested and fitted with industrial gauges and valves. By storing compressed air during low-demand periods and releasing it during spikes, the receiver tank stabilizes system pressure, reduces the compressor’s on/off cycling, and protects downstream equipment (tools, instrumentation, regulators) from shocks and pulsations. Our receivers are custom-engineered to suit your air system volume and pressure requirements (we offer a range of sizes, e.g. 500–5000+ liters, rated up to 10 bar). They feature safety relief valves, moisture drains, and provisions for level switches. Key Features: Large capacity for peak-demand buffering; rugged welded construction; powder-coated finish for corrosion resistance; NFPA-class pressure components; easy-drain fittings. Win Equipments’ air receivers are manufactured under strict quality standards (ISO 9001), ensuring leak-free, long-lasting performance in even the toughest industrial environments.
