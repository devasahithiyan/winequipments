# Find, size, compare and enquire

8 October 2026. User-authorized scope: calculator recommendation validation, product discovery, conditions-first product pages and contextual enquiries. Established Win identity; local extension, code-led. Operate mode for search/calculators/comparison; Read for product details. No new identity, no PDF regeneration or mailer reliability changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A customer can move from a plant requirement to a clearly qualified selection and send the exact duty without retyping it.
OWN-WORLD: Existing Archivo, green/ink, light surfaces, native inputs, genuine product imagery. No new raster assets.
STORY: Search or choose the task; read conditions; find/compare models; review and edit attached sizing details; send enquiry or explicitly share on WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Product index exposes search and family shortcuts. Calculator results visibly distinguish incomplete/invalid input, engineer review and preliminary catalogue match. Product sections lead with model conditions and sizing; photos/3D follow technical selection.
FORM: Inline progressive controls and comparison, no extra modal. Search filters existing products and uses names, series, model IDs and applications; empty results offer reset and advice. Comparison supports two or three models within one range. Contact details are never stored as selection state.
FINISH: Focused boundary/data-integrity tests, existing SEO suite, desktop/mobile/320px visual review and one confirmation pass for material fixes. Independent finish review and scoped documentation handoff. Existing URLs, model ratings, interactions and backend endpoint retained.

## Build sequence

1. Pure calculator rules and regression cases: positive/finite inputs, unambiguous load/flow mode, catalogue factor allowlists, desiccant rating conditions, tower wet-bulb/range guardrails, no confident model outside supported duties.
2. Search/family shortcuts and inline problem guide on the product index. Ordinary links and full product list work without JS.
3. Reorder product pages to specs/conditions → sizing or engineered-to-order requirements → supporting explanations/photos/3D. Add model comparison without changing specification values.
4. Shared validated selection context for calculator/model-finder/direct-model actions. Scope storage to the selected product; transfer through an explicit details link; visible editable/removable enquiry attachment; no contact-data persistence. Include context in existing form POST and user-initiated WhatsApp messages.
5. Rebuild public output and verify valid, invalid, out-of-range and missing-input paths; mobile/desktop search, comparison, context handoff/reload/product switches/removal, keyboard access and reduced motion. Do not submit real enquiries during testing.
