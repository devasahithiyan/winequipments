# Catalogue set and reader redesign

8 October 2026. Scope: the nine published PDFs, their reproducible print templates, metadata/previews, and the downloads PDF reader. Existing public PDF URLs, original archived brochures and engineering model values stay intact. Mode: Read, for plant engineers and procurement customers on phones and desktop.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One coherent engineering reference set. Every page is A4 portrait; model data precedes supporting explanations. Wide tables split by information group, repeating model IDs rather than shrinking into landscape pages.

**OWN-WORLD:** Existing Win green, green-black ink, white paper and Archivo. Compact brand line, large section titles, consistent table headers, clear units and repeating page footers. Genuine equipment imagery only.

**STORY:** Identify the product and listed range, jump to model/rating information, understand construction and selection, then confirm duty or request a drawing. The master covers all 23 current entries.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The PDF reader keeps the document title and download/close actions fixed. Desktop has section/page navigation at left and continuous pages at reading width. Mobile uses the full screen, fit-width pages, a compact page/zoom toolbar and collapsible contents.

**FORM:** Established brand extension, code-led. No identity replacement or concept tournament. Signature interaction: scrolling, contents links and the page selector stay synchronized; zoom preserves the current reading position.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Quality bar: all nine documents, all model rows and units retained; uniform portrait page boxes; no clipped text, orphan notes or missing sections; working internal links and outlines; selectable PDF/preview text; stable scroll and fit/percentage zoom; clear retry/fallback; bounded canvas memory; desktop/mobile visual evidence and regression tests. A layout edition does not claim engineering reapproval.
