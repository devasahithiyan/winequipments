## Phase 0: quick UI fix (1 session)

### 0.1 "Supplying plants including" logo strip
**Why it looks odd (measured from `images/clients/*.jpg`):**
1. Source logos are tiny JPGs, 67 px tall (89–216 px wide), so they are soft when shown at 40 px on retina screens.
2. Several have coloured or boxed backgrounds (`2.jpg` yellow, `6.jpg` light blue, KISCOL and Watertec grey boxes). `filter: grayscale(1)` turns those into grey rectangles, so some logos sit in boxes and others don't.
3. Every logo is forced to `height: 40px`, but aspect ratios run from 0.77 (KISCOL, near-square) to 3.2 (wide wordmarks), so square logos look tiny and wide ones look huge.
4. The label sits in the left-aligned `.container`, while the track runs full-bleed and is cut hard at the screen edges (AGNI and Aseptic are chopped mid-word). No edge fade.
5. `opacity: 0.7` on top of grayscale makes them washed out.

**Fix** (`src/site/assets/css/home.css` lines 68–84, `src/site/templates/pages/home.html` lines 93–103, `build.py` image step):
- In the build, convert each logo to a trimmed, background-removed PNG/WebP (near-white and flat-colour backgrounds knocked out, whitespace trimmed).
- Size by optical area, not fixed height: `height = clamp(28px, k / sqrt(aspect), 48px)`, so square and wide logos read as equal weight.
- Add `mask-image: linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #000 8%, #000 92%, transparent)` on `.marquee` so logos fade at the edges instead of being cut.
- Centre the label, make it an uppercase small label, and raise opacity to 0.85.
- **Owner:** send vector or high-res logos where possible (the permanent fix for softness).

**Done when:** no grey boxes, no logo visually twice the size of another, no hard-cut logos at 360 / 768 / 1440 px, PageSpeed unchanged.
