## 5. Plan, mapped to evidence

**A. Push the pages already on the edge of page 1** (cooling tower Coimbatore ~#12, air dryer Coimbatore ~#18, air receiver tank Coimbatore ~#19, air dryer Bangalore page 1). Strengthen the matching pages: title and heading with the exact phrase, a short model table, local detail, FAQ, internal links from blog posts and the home page.

**B. **C. Own the directories** that fill the first page (IndiaMART, Justdial, TradeIndia): complete every product listing with photos, the exact address, and links to your site. They take 1-4 slots; being present in them is a second route to the same buyer.

**D. Google Business Profile:** get re-verified, then add products, photos and weekly posts. Local searches show the map block first.

**E. Fix address consistency** on Facebook, Tradeindia, Aajjo and others (still showing old addresses).

**F. Measurement:** GA4 and Search Console are now on. In 2-4 weeks Search Console will show your real searches and impressions, replacing estimates.
