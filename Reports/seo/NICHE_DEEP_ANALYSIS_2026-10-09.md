# Niche deep analysis, 9 Oct 2026

Sources, all read today:
- Search Console API, 20 Sep to 7 Oct, query x page, country, device. Search Console hides rare queries, so its totals are lower than the daily totals (591 impressions here, 974 in the daily total).
- GA4 property 557291485, organic landing pages and event counts, 20 Sep to 9 Oct (9 Oct partial).
- Semrush batch keyword data, India database, 9 Oct (difficulty index, not the old KD% scale). Semrush's rank tracking is phone-based.
- Public pages in `public/`: titles, H1s, word counts.

Calculated figures show the formula. Semrush volumes are estimates and move between reads.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **Phone and desktop are very different.** Mobile: 433 impressions, average position 8.3. Desktop: 533 impressions, average position 22.1. Semrush tracks phone, so our "#1" results are phone-only. Desktop is where most B2B buyers search, so this is the largest gap we can measure.
2. **Demand is reaching us, but few clicks follow.** Clicks are 0 at positions 5 to 10 for the top moisture-separator terms. Example: "moisture separator" has 29 impressions at average position 8.8 and zero clicks. The guide's title ("Role & Position") does not say what a buyer searches for.
3. **Calculator searches land on the wrong page.** About 40 impressions on TR and chiller-calculation queries go to the "how to size a process chiller" article, which gets 1 click. The calculator page gets 14 impressions and average position 32. Intent is mismatched.
4. **The biggest volume term we almost ignore.** "Shell and tube heat exchanger" (8,100/mo, difficulty 28) gets 76 impressions at average position 71, and the guide gets zero clicks. We have 9 impressions on the plural at position 40.
5. **Organic visitors show intent but no submitted enquiries.** Organic: 6 form starts, 4 duty-finder uses, 3 quote-intent events, 0 quote submits, 0 calls, 0 WhatsApp clicks. The two quote submits and two call clicks are under Direct, which may include internal testing.
6. **Engagement is good.** Organic home page: 25 sessions, 84% engagement, about 9 minutes per session. Downloads, diesel-gensets and Chennai chiller pages also held visitors for minutes.

## 4. Actions, in order

1. **Calculator intent (priority 1).** On the process chiller sizing article, add a link to the chiller tonnage calculator with "chiller TR calculation formula" as anchor text. On the calculator page, make sure the H1 and first paragraph use "TR calculation formula". Re-check in 2 weeks. Cost: low, no new content.
2. **Moisture separator title (priority 2).** Change the guide title and H1 to lead with "moisture separator for air compressor". Keep the "Role & Position" section, but move it below the fold. Add a link from the product page. Re-check CTR in 2 weeks.
3. **Shell-and-tube manufacturers in India (priority 3).** Add an H2 and two FAQs that answer "who makes shell and tube heat exchangers in India" using only our real facts (Arasur works, engineered to order, owner-confirmed specs). No invented claims.
4. **Water chiller (priority 4).** The page's title says "Industrial Water Chiller Manufacturer". Add "water chiller" to the H1 subheads and one FAQ. This targets a 1,300/mo term with difficulty 4.
5. **Milk chiller and cooling-tower fills titles (priorities 6 and 7).** Small title edits only.
6. **Organic form drop-off.** Six organic users started the quote form and none submitted. Check the form on mobile (test it on a phone) and confirm the submit event fires on success.
7. **Mark key events in GA4.** The keyEvents metric is zero, so GA4 is not counting enquiries as key events. The account is not editable through the connector (see the 5 Oct memory note), so do this in the GA4 interface.
