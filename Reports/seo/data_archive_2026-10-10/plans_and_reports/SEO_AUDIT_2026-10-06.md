# SEO audit: winequipments.com, 6 Oct 2026

All numbers are measured, with the source in brackets. Search Console holds only about one week of data, so trends are not meaningful yet.

## Verdict

The site itself is in good shape: fast, clean, no broken pages, and it now covers 23 products, 31 city pages and 26 guides. **The bottleneck is Google, not the site.** Half the product and city pages are not indexed yet, the domain has almost no authority (Authority Score 2), and outside the brand name nothing ranks on page 1. That is normal for a site rebuilt in September; the work now is getting pages indexed and earning links.

## 3. Technical

**Semrush Site Audit (crawl of 4 Oct, 78 pages): health 93.**
- Errors: 149 structured-data errors. These were catalogue models marked up as separate Products without a price. **Fixed 6 Oct** (one Product per page with the model list as a property; awaiting deploy).
- Warnings: 189 "broken external links" are wa.me WhatsApp links (false positives); 72 unminified CSS/JS (the crawl predates the minified build); 1 low text-to-HTML page.
- Notices: 137 resources linked as pages (PDF and image links, harmless); 1 page with a single internal link; llms.txt "not found" (now live, returns 200).
- Zero 4xx/5xx pages, broken internal links, duplicate titles or descriptions, missing H1 or meta descriptions, canonical problems or redirect chains.

**Server checks (6 Oct):** http and www both 301 to https://winequipments.com in one hop; Brotli compression on; HSTS, nosniff and frame headers set; CSS/JS cached for a year, HTML revalidated; robots.txt allows all search and AI crawlers and lists the sitemap.

**Fixed 6 Oct (awaiting deploy):**
- `/index.html` returned 200 as a duplicate of the home page (Google showed it 4 times in results). Now 301 to `/`, and the same for `/folder/index.html`.
- Extensionless URLs such as `/products/refrigerated-air-dryers` returned 404. Now 301 to the `.html` page when it exists.
