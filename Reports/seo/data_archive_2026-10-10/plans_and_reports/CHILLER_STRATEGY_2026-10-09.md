## 3. One owner per term

Google splits a query between pages when two of ours compete (the cooling tower case). So each intent gets exactly one owner, and every other chiller page links to it.

- **Product, water and industrial chillers:** industrial process chillers page. Owns water chiller, water chiller machine, industrial chiller.
- **Product, air cooled and water cooled:** air cooled chillers page owns "air cooled chiller"; the water cooled option is covered on the industrial process chillers page. Decide with the owner whether to give "water cooled chiller" its own page.
- **Family, manufacturers and companies:** process cooling family page owns chiller manufacturers and chiller company in India.
- **Calculator:** chiller tonnage calculator owns TR calculation and the chiller formula.
- **Sizing:** how to size a process chiller article owns sizing and worked examples.
- **Prices:** industrial equipment prices article owns chiller price searches. Only add numbers the owner confirms (see section 5).
- **Cities:** city chiller pages (Chennai, Bangalore, Pune, Ahmedabad, Coimbatore, India) own location queries only. Each must stay distinct from the others.

## 4. Actions, in order

1. **Link the chiller pages to each other.** Each chiller product page (acid cooling, air cooled, anodizing, medical scan, milk, soda) should link to the industrial process chillers page and the calculator with the exact search phrase as anchor text. This is the quickest route to the product pages getting any impressions.
2. **Put the search phrase in each chiller product's title and H1.** Currently the products are named by application (e.g. "Acid Cooling Chillers"), which is correct for buyers but misses the generic searches. Keep the application name in the H1 and add "chiller" phrasing in the title.
3. **Add a short "water chiller machine" section to the industrial process chillers page** using only catalogue data: range 0.5 to 20 TR, air or water cooled, stainless steel tank and pump up to WCP 075, factory tested. Already partly done by the FAQ and title.
4. **Make the homepage stop competing for chiller queries.** The homepage takes "chiller manufacturers in Coimbatore" and "industrial chiller manufacturer in Coimbatore". Shift those to the Coimbatore location page (see the open item from earlier).
5. **Make the sizing and calculator path lead to a quote.** The calculator should offer "send this duty to our engineers" with the TR and temperatures pre-filled. Check the current behaviour before changing it.
6. **Build one comparison article: air cooled vs water cooled chiller price factors (no prices).** The current article covers the difference. A cost-factor section (power, cooling water, footprint) is useful and needs no invented numbers.
7. **Track chiller searches weekly.** Add 12 chiller terms to Semrush Position Tracking (the five water chiller terms are already there). Use Search Console's query list for the 37 chiller queries as the baseline: 80 impressions and 0 clicks.
8. **Off-site:** complete the chiller listings on IndiaMART and Justdial, with the real product photos, TR ranges and the company address. Justdial shows the chiller catalogue under the wrong category; that needs Justdial support (see the existing fix sheet).

## 5. What I need from the owner

- **Price bands per TR** for WCP chillers, with the source (quotation sheet or IndiaMART listing). IndiaMART shows "₹6,50,000 per tonne" for water chillers, which looks wrong, so it must not be copied. Without owner-confirmed bands the price searches (590, 320, 260/mo) stay unanswered.
- **Air cooled model specs** (TR range, power, refrigerant) to give "air cooled chiller" its own page.
- **Installation photos and one or two chiller case details** (customer type and duty, with the customer's permission). Without these, the product pages have no proof, and the articles stay generic.
- **Google Business Profile re-verification** to list chillers as a product. This stays on hold until verification (per CLAUDE.md).

## 6. Realistic expectations

- Products like "water chiller machine" (difficulty 4) can move into the top 20 within weeks of the changes above, and into the top 10 over a few months.
- Broad terms ("water chiller" at difficulty 24, "chiller machine") need months of links and proof. Ranking first for them can't be promised.
- The first measurable signal is impressions on the chiller product pages, expected within 2 to 4 weeks. Compare against the baseline in section 1 on about 20 Oct and again on about 3 Nov.
