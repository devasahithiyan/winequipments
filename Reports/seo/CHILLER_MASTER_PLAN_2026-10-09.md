## 3. Market research

### 3a. Local competitor: Gem Orion Machinery (Arasur, Coimbatore)
Source: [Gem Orion Coimbatore page](https://www.gemorion.in/industrial-chillers-in-coimbatore), [Gem Orion CHT page](https://www.gemorion.in/cyclic-industrial-chiller), search results on Coimbatore chiller makers.

- Joint venture of Gem Equipments India and Orion Machinery (Japan), in Arasur, Coimbatore. Same town as our works.
- Full model table per series: capacity in TR and kW, cooling type, refrigerant, compressor, pump flow and power, fan, tank, connections, dimensions and weight. The CHT air-cooled series runs from 1 TR to 50 TR; a water-cooled CHW100 is 13 TR.
- No prices on the pages checked. No per-model temperature ranges.
- Coimbatore page has an industry-specific section (textile, pumps and motors, foundries, auto components), a sizing section for Coimbatore conditions, a Coimbatore FAQ block, and quote, WhatsApp and phone calls to action.
- Self-claims that I could not verify: ISO 9001:2015 and "Japanese Orion technology".

What they do better: range coverage (up to 50 TR air-cooled), a complete spec table per model, industry-led local content.
What we do better (checked against our own pages): price transparency (indicative prices with source and date), a calculator that works out the TR, a sizing article with correction factors, a PDF datasheet, and the WCP spec table on one page.

### 3d. Marketplace price benchmark (indicative only)
Source: IndiaMART and TradeIndia listings, seen in search results 9 Oct. Listings are inconsistent and some are clearly wrong (a 50 TR listing at ₹20,000; a 5 TR at ₹45,000).

Calculated from listings that are internally consistent:
- Air-cooled and water-cooled packaged units in the 2 to 28 TR range were listed at roughly ₹42,000 to ₹63,000 per TR.
- Our listed prices, calculated from our own IndiaMART store (5 Oct): 1 TR ₹55,000 to ₹78,000 per TR; 10 TR ₹3,50,000, which is ₹35,000 per TR.

Conclusion: our listed prices sit inside the market range. They are not a reason to claim we are cheaper, because specs differ. Do not quote the marketplace figures on the site.

## 4. Gaps against the best of the market

1. **Range above 20 TR.** Our water chillers cover 0.5 to 20 TR; Gem Orion's air-cooled series reaches 50 TR. We need the owner to confirm whether we build 20 to 50 TR (multi-unit or engineered). Until then the site says "engineered to order" for large duty, which is accurate.
2. **Air-cooled spec table.** The air-cooled page has key specs but no model table. The owner must supply the air-cooled model data.
3. **Price pages.** Chiller prices sit in a multi-product article. The chiller price searches (590, 320, 110 a month) need their own owner.
4. **Internal links.** The chiller pages don't link to each other, to the calculator or to the price guide.
5. **Coimbatore positioning.** The homepage takes Coimbatore chiller queries while the Coimbatore chiller page gets none.
6. **Proof.** No chiller installation photos beyond two images, and no case details. Customer proof needs owner approval.
7. **Reviews.** IndiaMART rating 4.7 from 19 ratings is on the About page; it is not shown on chiller pages.
