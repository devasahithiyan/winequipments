## How to read the change
- Impressions rise before clicks. A rise in impressions on the chiller product pages within 2 to 4 weeks is the first sign.
- A position moving from over 30 to under 20 is a real gain; from 20 to 10 is the next gain.
- Do not compare pages with different dates. GSC data is always about 3 days behind.
