# Customer search research, 10 Oct 2026

What buyers of chillers, cooling towers, air treatment equipment and spares type into Google in India, what they see, and what that means for the website. Every number is measured, with its source. Nothing here assumes a price, spec or claim that is not in the catalogue or owner data.

## 1. Method and sources

| Step | Source | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phrase discovery | Google autocomplete (India), 30 seed products x 68 modifiers (letters, question words, cities, price, service, tons) | 6,933 unique suggestions; 4,794 relevant after removing consumer and non-India noise |
| Volumes | Semrush India database, about 400 clean buyer phrases in 4 batches | 197 phrases returned data; 70 have 100+ searches a month; the other phrases returned no data (below Semrush's floor) |
| Component demand | Semrush, 33 component phrases | 25 with volume |
| Live results | Google India search results, read 10 Oct 2026 for three key buyer searches | Price cards, "People also ask", related searches, competitor list |
| Our own searches | Search Console, 1 Sep to 6 Oct | 195 query-page pairs, sorted by intent |
| Real enquiries | IndiaMART September 2026 performance report (owner data) | 135 enquiries Apr to Sep; categories listed below |
| Competitor | Semrush organic database for Gem Orion (Coimbatore), 10 Oct | 7 ranking phrases, mostly brand |

Limits: autocomplete shows what people type, not how often. Semrush hides volumes below about 10 a month and underreports local searches. Google results can be personalised.

## 3. What buyers actually see on Google (10 Oct 2026, India)

**"industrial chiller price in india"**
- The page opens with a long carousel of marketplace product cards (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Aajjo), each showing a rupee price: from about ₹38,000 to over ₹25 lakh. Buyers see and compare prices before clicking.
- "People also ask": How much does an industrial chiller cost? A 1 ton chiller? A 2 ton? A 5-ton?
- Related searches: small industrial chiller price in India; chiller cost per ton in India; water chiller machine 1000 liter price; water chiller 500 liter price; water chiller price in India; water cooled chiller price.
- Justdial shows an average industrial chiller price directly in its snippet.

**"chiller manufacturers in coimbatore"**
- Results: IndiaMART directory, Justdial list ("Top Chiller Manufacturers in Coimbatore"), Gem Orion (Arasur), Reliance Engineering, Mekmax Engineers, Gireesh Industrial Chiller, AC Enterprises, Everestt Chillers. We are not on page 1.
- "People also ask": Who are the top 5 chiller manufacturers? Top 10 manufacturing companies in Coimbatore? How much does a 100 ton chiller cost? Which brand of chiller is best?
- Related searches: top, best, contact number, electric chiller manufacturers in Coimbatore, Industrial chiller manufacturers in India.

**"chiller for injection moulding machine"**
- Another price carousel: from about ₹22,500 to over ₹44 lakh, many by tonnage (2 TR, 5 TR, 10 TR, 80 TR, 150 TR, 200 TR).
- "People also ask": How much does a 1 ton chiller cost? What are the three types of chillers? What type of cooling system is used in injection molding? How much does a 500 liter water chiller cost?
- Related searches: chiller for injection moulding machine price; water chiller for injection moulding machine; cooling tower for injection moulding machine; best chiller for injection moulding machine.

What this tells us:
1. **Price appears inside the results.** Pages with a visible, sourced price and a clear Product listing compete better than pages without one.
2. **Questions are about price per size**, not just price.
3. **Local results are directories plus about six small local makers.** Our page 1 chances depend on the Google Business Profile, directory listings, and one strong Coimbatore page.
4. **"Water chiller 500 liter / 1000 liter"** are consumer or commercial drinking-water chillers, which is a different product from process chillers. We should not chase them.

## 6. Recommendations, in priority order

1. **Price by size (owner prices already on file).** Add 1 TR, 2 TR, 3 TR, 5 TR and 10 TR headings and FAQs to the chiller price guide, each answering "How much does a N TR chiller cost?" with the owner's IndiaMART figures and date. Add an injection moulding chiller price section for the 2 TR model (₹85,000). Targets chiller price (590), chiller 2 ton (140), chiller for injection moulding machine (110).
2. **Fans page (new).** One page for axial fans and cooling tower fans, using only the catalogue and IndiaMART listing data (aluminium alloy, 4 blades, 28 mm bore, 230 V). Targets axial fan (4,400), cooling tower fan (1,900), axial flow fan (880), axial fan price (210). It matches the real enquiry pattern. Owner confirms the fan specs and prices first.
3. **HSN and GST FAQ.** Chiller HSN code searches total about 340 a month (210 + 110 + 20). The owner or the company's accountant must confirm the correct HSN code and GST rate before we publish anything.
4. **Service and AMC page.** Chiller repair, service, AMC and "near me" total about 450 a month (170 + 110 + 140 + 30). If Win services chillers, a Coimbatore-focused service page is worth building. The owner must confirm what service is offered and where.
5. **"Chiller plant" and "chiller system" wording.** "Chiller plant" (5,400, difficulty 16, mixed with HVAC) and "chiller plant manufacturer" (140, difficulty 9) fit our packaged units only if the owner is comfortable calling them chiller plants. Decide the wording, then use it on the industrial chillers page.
6. **Answer the "People also ask" questions on the pages.** Use real catalogue facts for "What are the three types of chillers?", "What type of cooling system is used in injection molding?", "How much does a 100 ton chiller cost?" (only if we have a figure; otherwise say "engineered to order"). Do not answer "Which brand is best?".
7. **Local strength.** The local results are directories plus small makers. Our best levers are the Google Business Profile (after verification), the Justdial and IndiaMART listings, and the Coimbatore chiller page.
8. **Skip.** Consumer water chillers (500 and 1,000 litre), brand searches (Daikin, Carrier, Blue Star), and non-India suggestions.

## 7. Owner questions that unblock the plan
- Do you want a fans page, and are the fan specs and prices on IndiaMART correct?
- Which HSN code and GST rate should we publish for chillers?
- Does Win offer chiller service and AMC, and in which areas?
- May we call the packaged units "chiller plants" on the website?
